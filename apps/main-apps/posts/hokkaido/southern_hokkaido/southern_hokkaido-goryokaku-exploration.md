## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Overview:** Goryokaku, located in Hakodate, Hokkaido, is Japan’s first Western-style star-shaped fortress. Designed by scholar Ayasaburo Takeda based on European military architecture, construction began in 1857 by order of the Tokugawa Shogunate and was completed in 1864 to serve as the Hakodate Magistrate's Office (*Hakodate Bugyosho*) for northern defense and foreign diplomacy.
* **Historical Significance:** In 1868, during the restoration of imperial rule, former Shogunate forces led by Takeaki Enomoto and Toshizo Hijikata occupied the fort. It became the final battleground of the Boshin War (the Hakodate War). After the surrender of the Shogunate forces, the original office was dismantled. The site is currently preserved as a Special National Historic Site under the name Goryokaku Park.
* **Seasonal Data:** The park contains approximately 1,500 Yoshino cherry trees that bloom from late April to early May.

## 📍 Key Landmarks & Facilities

### 🌳 Goryokaku Park & Tower
![Goryokaku Park & Tower](/images/prefectures/southern_hokkaido/goryokaku_tower.webp)
* **Goryokaku Park:** Public park enclosed by stone walls and a moat. It features a **1.8 km walking and jogging trail** around the water perimeter. A wisteria trellis tunnel, over 100 years old and measuring **30 meters** in length, is located near the "Ni-no-Hashi" (Second Bridge) on the south side. Admission to the park ground is free.
* **Goryokaku Tower:** An observation tower standing adjacent to the fort. The top observation deck provides an overhead view of the star-shaped geometry.
* **Official Website:** https://www.goryokaku-tower.co.jp/en/

### 🏯 Hakodate Magistrate's Office (Hakodate Bugyosho)
![Hakodate Magistrate's Office](/images/prefectures/southern_hokkaido/hakodate_magistrate's_office.webp)
* **Overview:** A partial reconstruction of the original 19th-century Shogunate headquarters, rebuilt using traditional Edo-period materials, craftsmanship, and archaeological excavation data. The structure features Tohoku pine, Aomori hiba cypress, and Echizen roof tiles. The interior contains a 72-tatami mat grand hall, historical exhibit rooms, and the former magistrate's offices.

## ⏱️ Required Time

* **Standard Tour:** **2 to 2.5 hours** (Covers the Goryokaku Tower observation deck, walking across the bridge, entering the park, and touring the Magistrate's Office).
* **With Dining & Extended Walking:** **3 to 4 hours** (Includes sitting down for a meal at Lucky Pierrot or a nearby café, and walking along the full moat trail).

---

## 💡 Author’s Tips

For the best transportation experience, I highly recommend using the tram one way and taking the bus for the other! Mixing up your transportation options gives you a changing view of Hakodate’s distinct streetscapes through the windows, making your round trip doubly enjoyable. 

As for clothing, make sure to wear comfortable shoes like sneakers. While buses and trams get you close, you will still need to walk 10 to 18 minutes just to reach the park entrance, plus another 1.8 km if you decide to walk completely around the moat. Also, remember that Goryokaku just looks like a standard, leafy park from the ground level. To actually see the famous "Star Fort" shape, buying a ticket for the Goryokaku Tower is non-negotiable.
