## 🏔 Introduction

* **Overview:** Standing majestically at 1,898 meters, Mt. Yotei is one of Japan’s 100 Famous Mountains. For hikers looking to tackle this iconic volcano, the Makkari Trail on the south side is highly recommended as the most accessible route.Starting near the scenic Makkari Camp Site, this well-maintained trail offers a steady, demanding ascent that takes about four to five hours to the crater rim. The route is famous for its beautiful zoning; you will hike through dense, lush forests before emerging into breathtaking alpine meadows filled with vibrant wildflowers. At the trailhead, hikers can also experience the famous natural spring water of Mt. Yotei.

* **The ultimate reward awaits at the top:** A stunning panoramic view of Hokkaido’s vast landscape and the chance to walk around the massive volcanic crater. With its manageable terrain and rewarding scenery, the Makkari Trail is the perfect choice for a rigorous, full-day summer hike. It delivers a safe yet genuine mountain adventure for international trekking enthusiasts.

## ⚠️ Trail Regulations & Bear Safety

### 🥾 Environmental Rules
* **Trekking Poles:** To protect the mountain trails and alpine plants, you must attach rubber caps to the tips of your trekking poles.
* **Provisions:** There are no shops on the mountain. You must bring all your own water and food.

### 🐻 Brown Bear Safety Instructions
Brown bear sightings occur frequently across Hokkaido. To avoid encounters, you must follow these specific behavioral rules:
* **Group Travel:** Do not enter the mountains alone. Always hike in a group of two or more people.
* **Make Noise:** Always walk while making noise using tools like bear bells.
* **Waste Management:** Never leave or bury food or trash on the trail. You must pack out all items and carry them home.
* **Trace Awareness:** If you see any signs of a bear, such as tracks or droppings, turn around immediately.
* **Time Restrictions:** Do not hike during dark or low-light hours, such as early morning or dusk, in areas where bears are known to appear.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

Mount Yotei is a mountain for intermediate to advanced hikers. Climbing with light, casual gear is absolutely forbidden. Make sure to head out with proper mountain gear, including trekking poles. (If you travel light thinking you can just rent gear near Mount Yotei, there is a very high chance you will have trouble because there are very few rental shops around the area. Prepare and get your minimum required gear in such as Sapporo before you leave.) For the time required, the round trip takes about 10 hours, so you should plan to use a full day for this hike. Public transport to Kutchan Station is limited, and there is a free parking lot at the Mount Yotei trailhead, so I highly recommend renting a car from Otaru, Sapporo, or Chitose to access the mountain by car.
