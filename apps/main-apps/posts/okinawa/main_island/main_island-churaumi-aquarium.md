## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Overview:** Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium features regional marine life exhibits. The "Sea of Coral" tank is an open-air system utilizing natural sunlight. The main "Kuroshio Sea" tank holds whale sharks and reef manta rays. The "Deep Sea" section displays marine life from the deep waters surrounding Okinawa.
* **Official Website:** https://churaumi.okinawa/

## 📍 Key Exhibits & Features

### 🐋 Major Marine Exhibits
* **Whale Sharks:** The aquarium has kept whale sharks on display since March 1995 (starting under its previous facility name, the National Okinawa Memorial Park Aquarium), holding a long-term captive care record.
![Whale Sharks](/images/prefectures/main_island/churaumi_aquarium_whale_shark.webp)
* **Reef Manta Rays:** The facility managed the world's first successful group care and captive breeding of reef manta rays using a continuous fresh seawater supply system.
![Reef Manta](/images/prefectures/main_island/churaumi_aquarium_reef_manta.webp)
* **The Sea of Coral:** A **300 m³** tank with an open-top design to let in natural light. Most of the coral has been cultivated here for over 20 years. The tank includes local reef fish that naturally feed on seaweed and sea anemones to maintain the coral environment.
![Sea of Coral](/images/prefectures/main_island/churaumi_aquarium_sea_of_coral.webp)
* **Deep Sea Creatures:** Displays species collected from depths of 200 to 700 meters, including flame snapper (*Hamadai*), ruby snapper (*Nagatachikamasu*), and luminous shrimp, alongside explanatory panels and video screens.
![Deep Sea Creature](/images/prefectures/main_island/churaumi_aquarium_deep_sea_creature.webp)

### ☕ Café "Ocean Blue"
* **Overview:** A dining space located next to the "Kuroshio Sea" main tank. The café features 9 premium reservation tables directly next to the tank glass.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

If you want to cover everything, including the dolphin show, the Manatee Mansion, and the Sea Turtle Mansion, you should expect to spend about 4 hours here. Since you came all this way, you should take at least that much time. Day trips from Tokyo, Osaka, or Fukuoka are not realistic. You should set up your base in Naha and stay 1 night around Churaumi, which lets you also aim for other northern main island spots like Kouri Island. Another option is to stay 1 night in Naha and then travel here as a day trip from Naha. Otherwise, it will turn into a bullet trip and get painful for you.
