## 🏛️ Introduction

For those who ended their trip thinking "the sea is beautiful" in the main island of Okinawa or Ishigaki Island—if you want to see the true "blue," you must come to Miyakojima. 

What spreads out here is a breathtaking cobalt blue view that no longer feels real. Even travelers who have visited beaches all over the world are said to fall silent the moment they see the sea of this island. This "Miyako Blue" is already like a drug. 

This is not just a sightseeing resort. The beauty of this island is an overwhelming "violence of blue" that forces you to rewrite your brain's filter. Once you hack this island, the seas in other places will look a little blurry—the journey to taste such a dangerously beautiful view with all your five senses is about to begin.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🌴 Miyakojima City Tropical Botanical Garden
![Miyakojima City Tropical Botanical Garden](/images/prefectures/remote_islands/miyakojima_city_tropical_botanical_garden.webp)
* **Overview:** A large park covering **approx. 396,000 m²** that contains about 1,600 species of sub-tropical trees. It features a 400-meter avenue of deigo (tiger claw) trees that bloom red in early spring. The "Kingdom of Flowers" section, produced by garden designer Kazuyuki Ishihara, was completed in 2011.

### 🌉 Ikema Bridge
![Ikema Bridge](/images/prefectures/remote_islands/miyakojima_ikema_bridge.webp)
* **Overview:** Opened in February 1992 with a total construction cost of **10 billion yen**. It connects the main Miyako Island with Ikema Island and has a total length of **1,425 meters**.

### 🌅 Cape Nishi-Hennazaki
![Cape Nishi-Hennazaki](/images/prefectures/remote_islands/miyakojima_cape_nishi_hennazaki.webp)
* **Overview:** The westernmost and northernmost point of the main Miyako Island. It looks out toward Irabu Island, Ikema Island, and the Ikema Bridge. The cape features multiple wind power generation turbines as part of the regional Enetopia (coined word Energy + Utopia) project.

### 🦀 Shimajiri Mangrove Forest
![Shimajiri Mangrove Forest](/images/prefectures/remote_islands/miyakojima_shimajiri_mangrove_forest.webp)
* **Overview:** The largest mangrove community in the Miyako Islands, growing along a **1 km** deep inlet known locally as Batarazu. The Shimajiri Bridge (Sumajii-bashi) and walking paths were completed in September 2002 to allow public access.

### 🌉 Irabu Bridge
![Irabu Bridge](/images/prefectures/remote_islands/miyakojima_irabu_bridge.webp)
* **Overview:** Opened in January 2015, connecting the main Miyako Island with Irabu Island. It has a total length of **3,540 meters**, making it the longest toll-free bridge in Japan. It was ranked number one on TripAdvisor’s "Japan’s Bridges Ranking 2016."

## 💡 Author’s Tips

The required time for a trip here is 2 days and 1 night to 3 days and 2 nights, basing yourself in Naha on the main island of Okinawa. Since it is an island, if you have a rental car, you can generally get to the places you want to go right away. However, the impact of each sightseeing spot is very strong, and taking time for activities takes longer than you think. Above all, because it is a resort destination, spending your time in a relaxing way is the best choice for the whole trip.

---

## ⚠️ A Caution from the Author

From June to autumn, typhoons hit this area like crazy. The weather often gets rough, making it impossible to do the activities you want to do, so you should first understand this in advance. Also, there is a very high chance that your flight will stop and you will have to extend your stay. Therefore, during the summer season, setting up a schedule where you must fly back to your country immediately after visiting the Okinawa area, including Miyakojima as your final destination, is a big no-go. You will panic a lot, and you cannot beat nature, so basically, you will get into trouble.
