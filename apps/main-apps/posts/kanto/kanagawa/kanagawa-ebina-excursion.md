## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Geographical Position:** Located near the center of Kanagawa Prefecture, extending across a long north-south axis.
* **Administrative Borders:** Bounded by the Sagami River and Atsugi City to the west, Zama City to the north, Yamato and Ayase Cities to the east, and Fujisawa City and Samukawa Town (Koza District) to the south.
* **Topographical Division:** The western cliff of the Sagami Hills, historically known as the "Sagami Yokoyama Kyuri no Dote," divides the territory into western lowlands and eastern hilly zones.
* **Land Use and Development:** The western flatlands form an agricultural zone used for rice cultivation due to fertile soil and temperate weather conditions. The eastern hills undergo ongoing residential development and urbanization.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🚗 WEINS PARK Ebina
* **Operational Profile:** A multi-use commercial facility managed by an official Toyota dealership group, designed around automotive and outdoor experiences.
* **Facility Components:** Contains camping areas, barbecue sites, a dog run, public bathing facilities, a sauna, a specialty Yakiniku restaurant (Horinishi), and a cafe.
* **Schedule & Access:**
    * **Operating Hours:** 9:45 AM – 6:00 PM *(Individual facility hours vary)*.
    * **Regular Closures:** First and third Wednesdays of each month. *(if Wednesday is a national holiday, will be closed on Thursday)
    * **Access Route:** An 8-minute walk from Atsugi Station.
* **Official Website:** https://weinspark.jp/ebina/

### 🌳 Ebina Central Park
![Ebina Central Park](/images/prefectures/kanagawa/ebina_central_park.webp)
* **Spatial Placement:** Located directly in the commercial center of Ebina Station, surrounded by large-scale shopping complexes.
* **Internal Layout:** Divided into designated public squares, including Tsudoi (Assembly), Katarai (Talk), and Ikoi (Rest) plazas.
* **Access Route:** A 2-minute walk from Ebina Station (East Exit).

### 🚆 Romancecar Museum
![Romancecar](/images/prefectures/kanagawa/romancecar.webp)
* **Exhibition Catalog:** Displays historical models of the Odakyu Romancecar rolling stock alongside original train cars from the opening era of the Odakyu Line.
* **Interactive Elements:** Features a large-scale railway diorama of the Odakyu transit corridor and operational driving simulators.
* **Subsidiary Services:** Includes a museum cafe (free entry) serving menus themed around line history and regional ingredients from the Odakyu corridor.
* **Schedule & Access:**
    * **Operating Hours:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
    * **Regular Closures:** Second and fourth Tuesdays of each month *(Subject to temporary operational changes)*.
    * **Access Route:** Adjacent to Odakyu Line Ebina Station.
* **Official Website:** https://www.odakyu.jp/romancecarmuseum/

## 🍽️ Gourmet

### 🥮 Yamaguchiya
* **Operational History:** Established in 1937, maintaining original bean paste processing methods.
* **Core Product Inventory:**
    * **Renzan Monaka:** The signature wafer confectionery designed by the first-generation confectioner to emphasize the flavor profile of the internal red bean paste.
    * **Strawberry Daifuku:** A seasonal product selected as an official Ebina City specialty product. It uses fresh local strawberries, house-made fine bean paste, and soft Gyuhi (sweet rice cake) wrapping.
* **Metrics & Schedule:**
    * **Sales Performance Data:** Daily volume reaches approximately 3,500 units on peak season opening days.
    * **Seasonal Availability:** Strawberry Daifuku limited to the period from January 2 to approximately May.
    * **Operating Hours:** 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM.
    * **Regular Closures:** Tuesdays *(Operates if Tuesday falls on a public holiday; subject to temporary irregular closures)*.
    * **Access Route:** A 6-minute walk from Atsugi Station.

### 🍜 Ebina Hanten
* **Culinary Infrastructure:** Uses a house-made golden whole-chicken broth for its ramen and Tan-Tan noodle bases.
* **Agricultural Ingredient Sourcing:** 
    * **Pork Supply:** Utilizes "Chunoton," an original brand pork cultivated by the pig-farming division of Kanagawa Prefectural Central Agricultural High School.
    * **Tofu & Vegetables:** Sourced via Tomizuka Shoji and local Ebina agricultural networks.
* **Seasonal Menus:** Offers Yokohama Sanma-men (a regional vegetable-topped ramen) exclusively during the winter season.
* **Schedule & Access:**
    * **Operating Hours:** 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM.
    * **Regular Closures:** Saturdays, Sundays, National Holidays, and the New Year holiday period.
    * **Access Route:** An 11-minute walk from Ebina Station (East Exit).
