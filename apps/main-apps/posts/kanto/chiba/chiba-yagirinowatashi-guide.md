## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Operational Definition:** Yagiri no Watashi is the last remaining traditional farmer's ferry service on the Edogawa River, connecting Yagiri (Matsudo City, Chiba Prefecture) and Shibamata (Katsushika Ward, Tokyo).
* **Historical Background:** The Tokugawa Shogunate prohibited the construction of bridges over major rivers to protect the defense network of Edo. While official highway ferries faced strict military controls, the government permitted local operations for farmers who owned agricultural land on both sides of the river.
* **Historical Practicalities:** Historical records show that certain travelers who wanted to avoid official highway checkpoints disguised themselves as local farmers to cross the river using these agricultural boats.
* **Cultural Recognition:** The ferry site is widely recognized across Japan through its inclusion in the novel *Nogiku no Haka* (The Wild Daisy) and the famous traditional pop song *Yagiri no Watashi*.

## 💡 Author's Insights

![Yagiri no Watashi Ferry on the Edogawa River](/images/prefectures/chiba/yagirino_watashi_ferry_sub.webp)

* **The Ferry Experience:** This service provides a rare opportunity to cross a river on a traditional boat near the Tokyo metropolitan area. The route moves slowly through a quiet, green environment, offering a distinct contrast to the fast pace of modern urban life.
* **Directional Perspective and Extensions:**
    * **Visual Contrast:** Boarding the ferry from the Yagiri (Chiba) side offers a direct view of Tokyo Skytree across the water, showing a clear visual mix of urban architecture and rural landscape.
    * **Sairen-ji Temple Route:** A 20-minute walk from the Yagiri ferry landing leads to Sairen-ji Temple. The adjacent Nogikuen Observatory offers panoramic views covering the Yagiri agricultural fields, the flow of the Edogawa River, and the distant Tokyo skyline.

## 🗺️ Access

### 🚌 Bus and Rail Connections

* **Standard Route A (Via Bus):** JR Matsudo Station (West Exit) → Take Keisei Bus bound for Ichikawa Station → Get off at "Shimo-Yagiri" Bus Stop → Walk toward the Edogawa River embankment → Total walking time from the station area: Approx. 30 minutes.
* **Standard Route B (Via Train):** Hokuso Railway Yagiri Station → Walk toward the Edogawa River embankment → Total walking time: Approx. 30 minutes.
* **Special Weekend Service:** On Saturdays, Sundays, and select national holiday hours, a direct local bus route runs from the West Exit of JR Matsudo Station directly to the "Yagiri no Watashi" ferry landing bus stop.

### 🚨 Weather and Suspension Rules
* **Storm Closures:** The ferry service closes immediately without prior notice during stormy weather or high water levels.
* **Irregular Closures:** Irregular holidays may occur. Visitors are advised to verify operational status via telephone confirmation before departure.
