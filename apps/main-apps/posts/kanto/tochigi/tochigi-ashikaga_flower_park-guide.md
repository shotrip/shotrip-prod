## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Park Origins:** Established in 1968 as "Hayakawa Farm" in Horigome-cho (now Asakura-cho), Ashikaga City, Tochigi Prefecture. It relocated to its current site in Hasama-cho in 1997 because of city development.
* **Soil Purification System:** Built on an old wetland site. The foundation uses over 250 tons of charcoal layered across the grounds to clean the soil and help all plants grow better.
* **Park Scale:** Continuous building projects have brought the total park area to 100,000 square meters.
* **Relocation History:** The park's famous main wisteria tree (then 130 years old) was successfully moved by Konami Tsukamoto, Japanese first female tree doctor. This was the first successful relocation of a giant wisteria tree in Japan.

## 🏡 Garden Areas & Highlights

### 🍇 The Great Wisteria and White Wisteria Tunnels
![Great Wisteria Structure and White Wisteria Tunnel](/images/prefectures/tochigi/ashikaga_flower_park_tunnel.webp)
The main natural highlights include four great wisteria trees (three Noda Kyushaku and one Yae Kokuryu) and an 80-meter white wisteria tunnel. All of these are designated as natural monuments by Tochigi Prefecture. The seasonal "Story of the Wisteria - Great Wisteria Festival" runs for one month from mid-April to mid-May.

### 💡 "Garden of Illuminated Flowers" Light Display
![Illuminated Flower Garden Winter Light Display](/images/prefectures/tochigi/ashikaga_flower_park_lightup.webp)
A massive winter light display operating from late October. The park is officially chosen as one of the "Three Major Light Displays of Japan" by the Night View Convention Bureau and holds the number one ranking among night-view experts in Japan.

### 🎨 Seasonal Flower Layout
A continuous garden display designed to change colors through the year. The park sets a staggered blooming schedule for its wisteria pillars—moving from pink, purple, and white to yellow—to ensure flowers are on display across different weeks.

### 🚨 Park Rules
* **No Re-entry:** Ticket sales end 30 minutes before closing time. You cannot re-enter the park after you leave.
* **Shopping Area:** You can enter the gift shop area for free without buying an admission ticket.
* **Scheduled Holidays:** The park is closed on the third Wednesday and Thursday of February, and on December 31. The park may also close without notice for regular equipment checks.

## 💡 Author's Tips

![Ashikaga Flower Park Main Entrance](/images/prefectures/tochigi/ashikaga_shumai.webp)

* **Local Food Characteristics:** The traditional Ashikaga Shumai is very different from regular meat dumplings. It uses only minced onions and potato starch wrapped in a dumpling skin. It has a chewy, sticky texture, and people eat it with local savory sauce instead of soy sauce.
* **Optional Food Route:** A recommended optional route involves traveling 5 minutes by car or 15 minutes on foot from JR Ashikaga Station to Dainichi Chaya, located inside the Banna-ji Temple grounds. This shop serves local potato fried noodles (a unique local dish with soft potato pieces mixed into the noodles) and real Ashikaga Shumai.
