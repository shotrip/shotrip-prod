# Shinjuku Gyoen

Shinjuku Gyoen is a masterpiece of garden design. Whether you're visiting for cherry blossoms in spring or the vibrant colors of autumn, it never fails to impress.

## 🚊 How to Get There (Access & Navigation)
### 1. Nearest Stations
- **Station 1:** Shinjuku-gyoemmae Station (Tokyo Metro Marunouchi Line) - 5 min walk
- **Station 2:** JR Shinjuku Station (South Exit) - 10 min walk

### 2. Step-by-Step Route
- Take **Exit 1** at Shinjuku-gyoemmae Station.
- Walk straight along the main street until you see the Shinjuku Gate on your right.

---

## ⚠️ Local Manners & Crucial Rules
To enjoy your visit without any trouble, please keep these local rules in mind:

- **🚨 No Alcohol Allowed:** Unlike standard Japanese parks, bringing alcohol into Shinjuku Gyoen is strictly prohibited. There is a baggage check at the entrance.
- **🚫 No Play Equipment:** Playing with balls, frisbees, or drones is not permitted to preserve the peaceful atmosphere.
- **🗑️ Trash Manners:** There are very few trash cans. Please bring a small bag to take your garbage back to your hotel.

## 💬 Editor's Secret Tip (For Deep Travelers)
> **💡 Insider Tip:**
> If you want to avoid the massive crowds during the Cherry Blossom season, try entering through the **Sendagaya Gate** instead of the main Shinjuku Gate. It is usually much less crowded and offers a smoother entry!
