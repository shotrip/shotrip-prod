## 🏛️ Introduction

The symbol of Iwakuni City in Yamaguchi Prefecture, Kintai Bridge, is one of the three famous bridges in Japan and is a spectacular viewpoint. With cherry blossoms in spring, cormorant fishing in summer, and autumn leaves, postcard-like Japanese beauty spreads out no matter when you visit.
However, beyond this noble bridge, the most exciting and chaotic castle town hack awaits you.
In the square right across the bridge, for some reason, famous soft-serve ice cream shops compete with over 100 flavors. If you go further inside, you can warp to Iwakuni Castle at the top of the mountain by ropeway at once and look down at the great view of the Seto Inland Sea. It is the best paradise for half-day sightseeing, where traditional beauty and Japanese local B-grade entertainment are perfectly concentrated.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🌉 Kintai Bridge
![Kintai Bridge](/images/prefectures/yamaguchi/iwakuni_kintaikyo.webp)
* **Type and Size:** It is a wooden bridge with five arches. The total length is about 200 meters. It is one of the three famous bridges in Japan.
* **History and Dimensions:** It was built in 1673. The length is 193.3 meters and the width is 5 meters.
* **Seasons:** There are about 1,500 cherry trees, including Somei Yoshino, in spring. It has green leaves in summer and colored leaves in autumn.
* **Structure:** It uses a traditional wood joinery method for its arch structure.
* **Sightseeing Boat:** Visitors can look at the bridge from the river water on a sightseeing boat.
* **Light-up:** The bridge lights up during each season.

### 🌳 Kikko Park
![Kikko Park](/images/prefectures/yamaguchi/iwakuni_kikko_park.webp)
* **Type and History:** It is a large park built on the former residential lands of the Kikkawa family and their samurai servants. It is on the list of the "Top 100 Historical Parks in Japan."
* **Fountain:** The large water fountain in the center sometimes makes a rainbow.
* **Facilities:** The park has grass, tree shade, and benches for rest.
* **Nature:** The park has flower beds for different seasons, and large ginkgo trees turn yellow in autumn.
* **Inside the Park:** The former residential area contains Kikko Shrine, Kinun-kaku, a statue of Hozumi Tanaka (a military band leader and composer from Iwakuni), and a song monument for his music "Utsukushiki Tennen."

### 🏯 Kinun-kaku
![Kinun-kaku](/images/prefectures/yamaguchi/iwakuni_kinunkaku.webp)
* **History:** It was built in 1885 as an ema (wooden tablet) hall inside Kikko Shrine when the former residential land was opened as a park.
* **Style:** The building style looks like an old castle watchtower. It stands next to the moat of Kikko Park.
* **Architecture:** It is a two-story building with an irimoya-style roof and tiled roof sheets. The first floor has an earthen floor with wooden benches inside the walls. The second floor has a wooden floor with a balcony rail.
* **Art and Culture:** The front side has a painting of Shoki drawn by Bunki Ashino. There are no other major wooden tablets left today. Shoki is a god that protects people from diseases and was used as an amulet against smallpox in Iwakuni.

### 🦆 Kikko U-no-Sato
![Cormorant Image](/images/prefectures/yamaguchi/iwakuni_cormorant.webp)
* **Type and Opening:** It is a facility for raising cormorants used for the Kintai Bridge Cormorant Fishing. It opened on March 30, 2017.
* **Season:** The cormorant fishing near Kintai Bridge is said to have started in the Edo period. Today, it takes place every year from June 1 to September 10.
* **Fishing Method:** Cormorant fishing is an old fishing method recorded in the Kojiki. The masters and the birds work together with ropes under bonfire light to catch sweetfish.

### 🏰 Iwakuni Castle
![Iwakuni Castle](/images/prefectures/yamaguchi/iwakuni_castle.webp)
* **History and Location:** It is a mountain castle built in 1608 by Hiroie Kikkawa, the first lord of the Iwakuni domain. It sits on a mountain about 200 meters high, using the Nishiki River as a natural moat.
* **Reconstruction:** It had a Momoyama-style design with three stories and four floors, but it was pulled down seven years after construction due to the "One Castle per Province" law. The current castle tower was rebuilt in 1962.
* **View:** The top floor offers a view of Kikko Park, the castle town, Kintai Bridge, Iwakuni City, the Iwakuni air base, Iwakuni Kintai Bridge Airport, the Seto Inland Sea islands, Shikoku, and Miyajima.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

The required time for this itinerary is a day trip (starting from Hiroshima, Hakata, or Osaka). Once you access the Kintai Bridge area by bus, you can basically sightsee on foot or by using the ropeway, so you do not need to worry too much about the schedule of public transportation. Since there are many photogenic spots, you should plan a day trip expecting that taking photos will take time (and there is also the light-up of Kintai Bridge). Because it is overwhelmingly close from Hiroshima, it would be good to visit with the feeling of extending your trip for just one day using Hiroshima as your base.
