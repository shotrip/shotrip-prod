## 🏛️ Introduction

"For travelers who want to enjoy authentic trekking in Japan, but find it difficult to make a large-scale expedition..." Mount Daisen, a famous peak in Tottori Prefecture, serves as the perfect solution.

By using the local city of Yonago as your base, you can access the core area of the national park in just 30 to 50 minutes by car. The main "Natsuyama Climbing Course" is a well-maintained and easy-to-walk trail, yet it takes you through national natural treasures of plants and one of the finest beech forests in Western Japan, leading to a mountain summit with a breathtaking panoramic view. It is a highly satisfying trail for everyone from beginners to advanced climbers. With an abundance of support for rental wear and gear, it is truly an ideal destination for overseas hikers who want to experience authentic Japanese mountain climbing traveling light.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks
![Mount Daisen Trail](/images/prefectures/tottori/daisen_trail.webp)
### 🥾 Natsuyama Climbing Course
* **Course Profile:** 
  * The main climbing trail on Mount Daisen, highly recommended for first-timers, children, and beginners.
  * Home to the Daisen Kyaraboku yew trees (a national natural treasure) and one of the largest beech forests in Western Japan.
* **Estimated Hiking Time & Route Details:**
  * **Total Time:** Approx. 6 hours round-trip (3 hours ascent, 2.5 hours descent, plus rest periods). Walking speeds vary, so plan your schedule accordingly.
  * **Pacing Breakdown:** Natsuyama Trailhead → *(60 mins)* → 3rd Station → *(60 mins)* → 6th Station → *(60 mins)* → Mount Daisen Summit.

**Gear Rental Service (Daisen Tourism Bureau):**

* **Hours:** 8:30 - 17:00 *(Same-day rentals only. Items must be returned by 17:00)*.
* **Important Note:** On-site rental is available, but advance booking is highly recommended to guarantee stock availability.
* **Step-by-Step Rental Flow:**
  1. **Plan Your Trip:** Once your travel dates are set, choose the items you need from the official product list.
  2. **Make a Reservation:** Book your items in advance by phone or via the online reservation form.
  3. **Pick up on Site:** Visit the Daisen Tourism Information Office on the day of your hike to receive your items. Rental fees are paid at the time of pickup.
  4. **Return the Gear:** Items must be returned on the same day by 17:00.

### ♨️ Goen Yuin Day-Trip Hot Spring
![Hotspring Image](/images/prefectures/tottori/daisen_hotspring.webp)
* **Details:**
  * A modern hot spring facility located on Mount Daisen, which is also traditionally known by its alternative name "Hinokamitake" (Mountain of the Fire God).
  * **Premium Water Quality:** Features spring water drawn from 1,200 meters underground with an oxidation-reduction potential of -320mV. This reading is considered one of the largest in Japan, attracting attention for its fatigue recovery and healing benefits.
  * **Bath Types:** Includes "Kami-no-yu" (an atmospheric indoor cave-like bath lit by bonfires) and a scenic outdoor bath with views of the mountain and seasonal gardens.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

It is entirely possible to do a day-trip climb by renting a car and using Yonago as your starting point. Since climbing the mountain via public transportation is not realistic, you should stay the night before in Yonago, rent a car, and head out for your day-trip climb. (If your schedule allows you to reach Yonago from the Okayama or Tokyo side early in the morning, it is not completely impossible to skip staying the night before, but it will likely turn into a hectic, busy, and exhausting trip.)

---

## ⚠️ Author’s Request

Please watch out for bears as they do appear. A bear bell is mandatory. Also, even though this is a course for beginners, you will be reaching quite a high altitude, so please make sure to properly prepare genuine mountain climbing gear.
