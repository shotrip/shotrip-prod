## 🏛️ Introduction

Kojima is the ultimate paradise for unique fashionistas, where Japanese denim culture exploded into existence.

While walking through the streets to hunt for your very own pair of vintage jeans is a given, you can also extend your steps to "Mount Washuzan," which offers a panoramic view of the beautiful islands scattered across the Seto Inland Sea, and capture the breathtaking scenery of the massive Great Seto Bridge on camera. Right here lies a one-of-a-kind local hack where street culture intersects with the vast nature of Seto Inland Sea, offering something you won't find in just any typical tourist spot.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### ⚓ Kojima Sightseeing Port
![Kojima Sightseeing Port](/images/prefectures/okayama/kojima_sightseeing_port.webp)
* **Overview:** Constructed at the former Nozaki Beach as a dedicated tourist boat terminal to coincide with the opening of the Great Seto Bridge in 1988. The terminal serves as the departure and arrival point for the Seto-Ohashi Bridge Sightseeing Cruise, which offers panoramic views of the Seto Inland Sea landscape. It also operates special seasonal cruises, including the "Seto Inland Sea Sunset and Mizushima Industrial Complex [Factory Night View Cruise](https://www.okayama-kanko.jp/event/detail_12454.html)."

### 🛖 Old Shimotsui Shipping Agent Museum
* **Overview:** A specialized historical museum featuring a restored shipping agent residence from the Meiji period. The complex exhibits historical materials related to the Shimotsui port district, displays local merchant architectural styles, and sells regional specialty products. An internal restaurant serves seafood from the Seto Inland Sea.
* **Official Website:** https://shimotsui-kaisen.com/

### ⛰️ Mount Washuzan
![Mount Washuzan Viewpoint](/images/prefectures/okayama/kojima_washuzan.webp)
* **Overview:** Positioned at the southern tip of the Kojima peninsula, this site serves as a representative landscape viewpoint within the Setonaikai (Seto Inland Sea) National Park and is a documented sunset viewing location. The mountain summit, named "Shoshuho," sits 133 meters above sea level and provides a 360-degree view of the archipelago. The geographical name originates from its visual resemblance to an eagle expanding its wings to take flight when viewed from a distance.

### 👖 Kojima Jeans Street
![Kojima Jeans Street](/images/prefectures/okayama/kojima_jeans_street.webp)
* **Overview:** While the Kojima district has operated as a textile manufacturing hub since the Meiji period, it is prominently known for executing the first domestic production of denim jeans in the 1960s. This specialized street spans approximately 400 meters through the Ajino Shopping District, running from the Former Nozaki House residential property to the Ajino No. 2 Park. The street project was organized by a local manufacturer council and the Kojima Chamber of Commerce to utilize vacant storefronts. It features a concentrated row of independent domestic denim brand shops, specialty designers, and local cafes.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

* The total time required is about half a day. If you are going to visit Kojima, it is much better to base yourself out of Okayama. This is because there are plenty of other worthwhile sights to see nearby, such as Kurashiki. 
* Please note that trying to make the trip directly with Osaka or Kyoto as your base will result in a large portion of your day being eaten up by travel time. That being said, if Kojima is your sole objective, it is completely fine to plan it as a day trip from Osaka, Kyoto, or Hiroshima. Additionally, the Kojima district faces the Seto Inland Sea, giving it one of the mildest and most comfortable climates in Japan. Bicycle rentals are available right at Kojima Station, so you should definitely make active use of a bike to tour around Kojima's nature, history, and jeans. (There are buses available too, so do not worry!)
