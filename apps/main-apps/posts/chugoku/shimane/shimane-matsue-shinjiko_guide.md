## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Geographical Classification:** Matsue is a city situated on the shores of Lake Shinji and is characterized by its network of water channels.
* **Tourism Designation:** Lake Shinji is officially selected as one of the "Top 100 Sunsets in Japan."
* **Lakeshore Data:** Due to this designation, crowds of tourists and local photographers gather along the shoreline during the evening sunset hours.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🌲 Shiragata Park
![Shiragata Park](/images/prefectures/shimane/matsue_shiragata_park.webp)
* **Location & Geography:** Situated on the south side of the Shinji-ko Ohashi Bridge. The park is divided by a prefectural road into a western section (Japanese style, planted with pine trees) and an eastern section (grass lawn field with mixed vegetation).
* **Infrastructure:** Features a reinforced shoreline revetment designed for public water access. The park has been utilized as a filming location for television dramas.
* **Access:** 15-minute walk from JR Matsue Station.

### 🌉 Shinji-ko Ohashi Bridge
![Shinji-ko Ohashi](/images/prefectures/shimane/matsue_shinjiko_ohashi.webp)
* **Location:** Spans the easternmost edge of Lake Shinji near the point where the lake connects to the Ohashi River. 
* **Access:** 15-minute walk from JR Matsue Station.

### 🖼️ Shimane Prefectural Museum of Art
![Shimane Prefectural Art Museum](/images/prefectures/shimane/matsue_shimane_prefectual_art_museum.webp)
* **Exhibits:** Displays paintings, crafts, and photography under the theme of harmony with water. Hosts multiple special exhibitions annually.
* **Lobby Infrastructure:** The 1F glass-enclosed lobby has no admission fee and functions as a viewing area for Lake Shinji.

### 🌳 Kishi Park
* **Location & Naming:** A waterfront public park located directly in front of the Shimane Prefectural Museum of Art. It was named to commemorate Seiichi Kishi, a Matsue native who contributed to the modern sports industry.
* **Composition:** Contains the outdoor architecture of the museum and a collection of sculptures installed within the park grounds.
* **Access:** 20-minute walk from JR Matsue Station.

## 🍽️ Local Gourmet

### 🦪 Shijimi Clams (Corbicula japonica)
![Shijimi Clams](/images/prefectures/shimane/matsue_shijimi_clams.webp)
* **Biological Context:** Harvested from Lake Shinji, which is classified as a brackish lake containing a mix of seawater and freshwater. 
* **How to eat:** Characterized by large sizes and thick meat. Distributed as topping for miso soup or processed into items like tsukudani.

### 🥢 Restaurant: [Ajidokoro Han](https://han-matsue.jimdofree.com/)
* **Profile:** A Japanese restaurant operating for over 20 years. The menu focuses on regional Japanese cuisine utilizing local ingredients from Shimane Prefecture and the "Seven Delicacies of Lake Shinji."
* **Menu:** Open for both lunch and dinner. The primary specialty is Kamameshi (pot rice), featuring options such as the Lake Shinji shijimi clam variant. Also offers set meals (Gozen) that include raw sliced fish (sashimi) and deep-fried items (tempura).
* **Operation Note:** Preparation of Kamameshi requires advanced cooking time; placing orders via telephone prior to arrival is recommended.
* **Access:** 15-minute walk from JR Matsue Station.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

The required time for this itinerary is about half a day, starting and ending at JR Matsue Station. Since all the scenic sunset spots and culinary highlights are concentrated around Matsue Station, my personal recommendation is to use the station as your base and explore the area on foot as a leisurely stroll. 

If you are coming from the Okayama, Hiroshima, or Osaka areas, the travel time alone will take 3 to 4 hours. While a day trip is technically possible, considering that you will be watching the sunset, returning to your original base afterward would make for an extremely late and exhausting night. Therefore, it is much better to shift your accommodation base directly to Matsue for sightseeing. Furthermore, this region is known as the San'in area within the Chugoku region, and it possesses an entirely different economic and cultural sphere compared to Okayama or Hiroshima. To truly immerse yourself and experience this regional contrast, moving your base to Matsue is highly recommended. (Staying in Matsue also provides excellent and convenient access to other famous nearby destinations, such as Izumo Taisha Grand Shrine.)
