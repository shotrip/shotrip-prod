## 🌳 Introduction

Oirase Gorge is a protected 14-kilometer river valley located within Aomori Prefecture, serving as part of the Towada-Hachimantai National Park. Geographically, it forms the only natural outflow of Lake Towada, which sits on the regional border with Akita Prefecture. The designated gorge section begins at Nenokuchi by the lakefront (400 meters above sea level) and drops down to Yakeyama (200 meters above sea level), where the main river joins the Tsuta River coming from the Hakkoda mountain range.

The national government maintains strict environmental controls over the area, designating it as a Special Protection District, a National Natural Monument, and a Special Place of Scenic Beauty. Despite these preservation laws, the valley features an accessible layout where a public vehicle highway and a dedicated dirt walking trail run directly alongside the stream. This structure allows visitors to observe the preserved old-growth forest up close without demanding advanced wilderness trekking experience.

## 📸 Highlights

The riverside path introduces visitors to a variety of distinct water flows and natural formations as the elevation drops through the forest.

### 🌿 Samidare Flow (Sanran no Nagare)
![Oirase Stream Rushing Water](/images/prefectures/aomori/sanran_no_nagare.webp)
This river segment is defined by a natural split in the channel layout where the main body of water divides into three distinct streams. The current here moves smoothly past the mossy rock formations situated in mid-stream, with the surface directly reflecting the dense foliage of the overhanging forest trees. Various small plants and ferns grow natively on top of the boulders anchored within the flowing water.

### 🌊 Ashura Flow (Ashura no Nagare)
![Oirase Stream Rushing Water](/images/prefectures/aomori/ashura_no_nagare.webp)
This high-velocity section serves as one of the central landmarks along the walking route and is heavily utilized in regional travel advertisements. Named after an ancient deity of combat, the site features a narrow channel where the river crashes aggressively over large boulders scattered across the path of the stream. The immediate area is entirely enclosed by deciduous broad-leaved trees that block direct sunlight.

### ⛰️ Kumoi Falls (Kumoi no Taki)
![Oirase Stream Rushing Water](/images/prefectures/aomori/kumoi_fall.webp)
This 20-meter-high waterfall drops in three distinct stages down a vertical cliff face before emptying into the primary Oirase riverbed. A short spur path branches off the main pedestrian trail, allowing hikers to walk straight to the lower basin underneath the waterfall. The ground around the viewing area remains damp from the continuous water spray.

### 💧 Choshi Great Falls (Choshi Otaki)
![Oirase Stream Rushing Water](/images/prefectures/aomori/choshi_great_fall.webp)
Measuring 15 meters in width and featuring a vertical drop of 7 meters, this is the largest waterfall located on the main channel of the Oirase River. Because its height completely prevents fish from migrating upstream from the river into Lake Towada, the feature is traditionally known by the alternative name "Fish-Stop Waterfall." It marks a major geographic transition point along the upper section of the trail.

### 🚢 [Lake Towada Ferry Operations](https://www.toutetsu.co.jp/ship.html)

![Oirase Stream Rushing Water](/images/prefectures/aomori/lake_towada_ferry.webp)

Sightseeing boat routes operated by the Towada Kanko Dentetsu company allow travelers to connect their gorge hike with open-water transit across Lake Towada.

#### 1. Yasumiya to/from Nenokuchi Route (50-Minute Transit)
This route connects the tourism settlement of Yasumiya directly with the gorge trail entrance at Nenokuchi. The vessel sails around the Nakayama Peninsula, through the inner lake basin (Nakinoumi), and past the cliffs of the Ogura Peninsula.
* **Sailing Windows**:
  * Departures from Yasumiya: 9:30 AM – 2:45 PM
  * Departures from Nenokuchi: 11:00 AM – 1:30 PM
* **Fares**: 1,760 yen for adults / 880 yen for children
* **Upper Floor**: 550 yen for adults / 330 yen for children

#### 2. Yasumiya Loop Route (50-Minute Circuit)
This round-trip cruise departs from and returns to the same pier in Yasumiya. The itinerary covers the waters surrounding the Nakayama Peninsula and the inner lake before crossing the deep central section of Lake Towada.
* **Sailing Windows**:
  * Operating Hours: 8:45 AM – 2:40 PM (*Depending on Seasons)
* **Fares**: 1,760 yen for adults / 880 yen for children
* **Upper Floor**: 550 yen for adults / 330 yen for children

## 🍽️ Gourmet
![Oirase Stream Rushing Water](/images/prefectures/aomori/towada_barayaki.webp)

### 🥩 Recommended Restaurant: Kamikochi Shokudo
This local eatery is a highly famous dining spot specializing in Towada Barayaki, a beloved regional comfort food. The clear standout menu item here is the Beef Barayaki Set Meal, priced at 1,300 yen. The dish is served in a traditional, highly interactive style where thin slices of beef and a mountain of sweet onions are cooked on a dedicated tabletop iron griddle directly by the diner. This layout allows you to stir-fry the ingredients to your exact preference, caramelizing the rich, savory soy-based sauce right at your table.

## ⏱️ Total Travel Time

Walking schedules through the valley vary depending on your chosen entry point and personal pacing. Itineraries can be organized using these two standard distance and walking time measurements:

* **The Complete Valley Course (Yakeyama to Nenokuchi)**: **Approximately 5 hours on foot**. This route spans the entire 14-kilometer length of the managed river trail. It is organized for hikers who wish to follow the continuous geographical progression from the lower forest basin up to the lake entrance.
* **The Selected Section Course (Ishigedo to Nenokuchi)**: **Approximately 2.5 hours on foot**. This popular 9-kilometer option skips the lower section of the valley to focus entirely on the upper reaches of the stream, which contain the highest concentration of rapids, major pools, and waterfalls.
