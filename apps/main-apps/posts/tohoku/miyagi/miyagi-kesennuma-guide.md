## 🏛️ Introduction

Kesennuma City is located at the northeastern end of Miyagi Prefecture, facing the Sanriku Coast. The area is adjacent to the Sanriku fishing ground, where warm and cold currents meet. Kesennuma Port handles the landing of commercial species including skipjack tuna, Pacific saury, coastal tunas, and swordfish throughout the year.

The harbor infrastructure accommodates large pelagic and coastal commercial fishing vessels. The surrounding coastal landscape forms part of the Sanriku Fukko National Park, characterized by a ria coastline with complex inlets. Key geographical features of the municipality include the Karakuwa Peninsula and Kesennuma Oshima, which is one of the largest inhabited islands in the Tohoku region.

## ⏳ Historical Framework

* **2011 Seismic Event:** On March 11, 2011, the municipality sustained damage from tsunami inundation and fires during the Great East Japan Earthquake.
* **Archival Facilities:** Specific structural remnants have been preserved as official Earthquake Memorial ruins to document the physical impact of the disaster and the subsequent reconstruction process.

## 🪼 Landmarks & Highlights

### ⛩ Shinmyozaki Cape / Ukimido & Ebisu Statue
![Shinmyozaki Cape](/images/prefectures/miyagi/shimyozaki_ukimido.webp)
* **Geographical Profile:** A promontory overlooking the inner basin of Kesennuma Bay. It serves as an ecological site known as "Hyakujuen" due to its botanical diversity, which includes a grove of *Mokugenji* (goldenrain trees) at their northernmost natural limit.
* **Structures:** Contains the Isuzu Shrine, the Igari Shrine dedicated to seaweed aquaculture pioneer Shinbei Igari, a Nihon Hakkei monument with poetry by Takeo Kumagai, and a haiku stone monument dedicated to Matsuo Basho.
* **Infrastructure:** A red maritime pedestrian boardwalk traces the inner bay. The Ukimido pavilion is equipped with a nocturnal lighting system.
* **Transit:** Board the local Oshima Bus Line from JR Kesennuma Station, get off at the "Sakaguchi" stop, and walk 2 minutes (Total transit time: 11 minutes).

### 🏔 Mount Tokusenjo
![Mount Tokusenjo](/images/prefectures/miyagi/tokusenjo.webp)
* **Geographical Profile:** A peak with an elevation of 711 meters, located on the boundary between Kesennuma City and the former Motoyoshi Town.
* **Botanical Density:** The slopes support a colony of approximately 500,000 wild *Yama-tsutsuji* (torch azaleas) and *Renge-tsutsuji* (Japanese azaleas) across a continuous 50-hectare area.
* **Seasonal Period:** Full bloom occurs annually between mid and late May.
* **Transit:** Taxi transport from JR Kesennuma Station to the mountain base costs approximately 5,100 yen one-way.

### 🍽️ Mishione Yokocho
![Mishione Yokocho](/images/prefectures/miyagi/mishione_yokocho.webp)
* **Structural Design:** A commercial cluster composed of specialized trailer house units located near the Kesennuma Fish Market and the "Umi-no-Ichi" facility.
* **Tenant Details:** The complex contains a public bathhouse (sento), local diners, a bar, a ramen shop, an Islamic prayer space (Musholla), and restaurants serving Mexican, Okinawan, and Indonesian cuisines.
* **Operations:** Serves as the venue for the "Minato de Marche" morning markets and scheduled events focused on culinary programs and mobile saunas.
* **Transit:** Board the City Loop Bus from JR Kesennuma Station and get off at the "Umi-no-Ichi-mae" stop (Total transit time: 13 minutes).

### 🧺 Kesennuma Sakana Ichiba
![Kesennuma Fish Market](/images/prefectures/miyagi/kesennuma_fish_market.webp)
* **Commercial Function:** A waterfront commercial facility distributing fresh landings from the adjacent fish market and regional processed goods, including concentrated shark fin soup.
* **Key Product:** "Aburi Sanma" (seared Pacific saury), which received the Emperor's Trophy at the 42nd Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries Festival.
* **Facilities:** Contains an attached restaurant serving sashimi rice bowls (kaisendon).
* **Transit:** City Loop Bus to the "Minatomachi" stop.
* **Operating Hours:** 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM (Open daily).

### 🐟 Kori no Suizokukan (Ice Aquarium)
![Ice Aquarium](/images/prefectures/miyagi/ice_museum.webp)
* **Technical Specifications:** An indoor environmental chamber maintained at a constant operating temperature of -20°C. It utilizes local industrial ice-making technologies to display 700 specimens across 40 regional marine species encased inside transparent ice blocks frozen over a one-week period.
* **Exhibits:** Includes projection mapping sequences modeling the Sanriku marine ecosystem and ice sculptures of the regional mascot, Hoyabo-ya. Thermal suits are provided for entry.
* **Transit:** City Loop Bus to the "Umi-no-Ichi-mae" stop.
* **Operating Hours:**
  * May to September: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM *(Final entry 4:50 PM)*
  * October to April: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM *(Final entry 4:40 PM)*
  * *Note: Operations advance to 9:00 AM and extend to 5:30 PM on Saturdays, Sundays, and National Holidays.*
* **Closures:** Aligned with Umi-no-Ichi facility closures.
* **Admission Fees:** Adults: 600 yen / Elementary Students: 400 yen / Preschool (Ages 4+): 100 yen.
* **Website:** https://www.uminoichi.com/iceaquarium/

### 🦈 Shark Museum
![Shark Museum](/images/prefectures/miyagi/shark_museum.webp)
* **Overview:** A specialized elasmobranch museum located on the second floor of the "Umi-no-Ichi" facility, utilizing data from Kesennuma's shark landings.
* **Exhibits:** Reorganized in April 2024. Features an 8-meter replica of a basking shark constructed in cooperation with the Okinawa Churaumi Aquarium.
* **Transit:** City Loop Bus to the "Umi-no-Ichi-mae" stop.
* **Operating Hours:**: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM *(Final entry 4:00 PM)*
* **Closures:** Irregular closures *(Open daily from July through December).*
* **Admission Fees:** Adults: 600 yen / Elementary Students: 400 yen
* **Website:** https://www.uminoichi.com/museum/index.html

## 🍽️ Gourmet
![Fukahire](/images/prefectures/miyagi/fukahire.webp)
* **Distribution Centers:** Concentrated within Mishione Yokocho and Kesennuma Sakana Ichiba.
* **Specialty Products:** Menus focus on fresh Sanriku marine catches, with shark fin (fukahire) serving as the primary commercial maritime product of the city.

## ⏱️ Total Estimated Time Required

* **Day-Trip:** Approximately 12 hours of total duration is required when executing a single-day round trip from Sendai Station.
* **Overnight:** An overnight stay allows for travel to the outer sections of the rias coastline, beaches, and isolated islands of the Sanriku area.
