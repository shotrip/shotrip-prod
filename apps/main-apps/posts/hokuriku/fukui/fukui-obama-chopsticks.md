## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Contemporary Product Design:** Wakasa lacquerware chopsticks incorporate specific modern structural designs to match diverse culinary styles.
* **Functional Innovations:** Features non-slip textured tips and varied handle materials, such as wrapped threads or fabrics, to improve grip stability on small or smooth food surfaces.
* **Aesthetic Extensions:** Integrates contemporary color selections, including pastel tones and casual patterns, to complement both traditional Japanese and Western-style table settings.

## 📜 Historical Development & System Specifications

* **Origins:** Established during the early Edo period when Matsuura Sanjuro, an official lacquer artisan for the Obama Domain, created designs inspired by the seabed, referencing Chinese lacquerware techniques.
* **The Golden Era:** Spanning the mid-to-late Edo period, artisans developed over 200 distinct decoration methods. These included foil-pressing and polishing techniques using mother-of-pearl (Raden) and eggshells, often combined with gold/silver dust lacquer painting (Makie).
    * **Mother-of-Pearl (Raden):** A technique where the iridescent inner layers of seashells are cut into specific shapes, polished, and embedded into the lacquered surface.
    * **Gold/Silver Lacquer (Makie):** A technique where designs are painted with wet lacquer and dusted with gold or silver powder before being polished to a high gloss.
* **Protection:** In 1634, Lord Sakai Tadakatsu officially named the craft "Wakasa-nuri" and established domain-wide protective laws that prohibited the transfer of these technical skills to other region. 
* **National Promotion:** In 1653, Tadakatsu deployed Sanjuro and local wood-turners to Edo (Tokyo) for a 7-month demonstration to promote Wakasa lacquerware nationwide. Sanjuro was rewarded with silver and rice, eventually receiving a permanent official stipend in 1569.

## 🗺️ Access

### 🚆 Rail & Bus Routing to Obama Station
* **From Tokyo & Nagoya (via Tokaido Shinkansen):** Tokaido Shinkansen → Transfer at Maibara → Tsuruga Station → JR Obama Line → Obama Station. (Total travel time: Approx. 4 hours from Tokyo, 2 hours from Nagoya).
* **From Tokyo, Nagano, Kanazawa & Fukui (via Hokuriku Shinkansen):** Hokuriku Shinkansen → Transfer at Tsuruga → JR Obama Line → Obama Station. (Total travel time: Approx. 4 hours from Tokyo, 1 hour 10 minutes from Tsuruga).
* **From Osaka & Kyoto (via Kosei Line):** JR Kosei Line to Omi-Imazu Station → Local Bus Route → Obama Station. (Total travel time: Approx. 2 hours 30 minutes from Osaka, 1 hour 40 minutes from Kyoto).

### 🚗 Local Station Transfer
* **From Obama Station:** The destination is a 10-minute drive from JR Obama Station. Taxis and rental bicycles are available at the station terminal.

## 🥢 Craft Centers
![Wakasa Lacquerware Chopsticks Sub](/images/prefectures/fukui/wakasa_lacqueware_chopsticks_sub.webp)

### 🏛️ Chopstick Museum: WAKASA (Hashi no Furusatokan WAKASA)
* **Retail Showroom:** Functions as the official cooperative showroom representing local wood suppliers, manufacturers, and retailers. It maintains an active inventory of approximately 3,000 distinct chopstick varieties available for direct purchase.
* **Special Discount Event:** The facility applies a 50% discount to all products storewide annually on August 4 (designated as National Chopstick Day).
* **Wakasa-nuri Polishing Workshop:** Features a dedicated public craft station where visitors polish layers of colored lacquer, natural pearl, and eggshell to reveal unique, individualized patterns.
* **Workshop Details & Group Sizes:**
    * **Workshop Fee:** 1,100 JPY (tax included).
    * **Activity Component:** Polishing a set of chopsticks embedded with natural pearl elements.
    * **Duration:** Approximately 10 minutes.
    * **Capacity Limit:** Up to 10 participants per session.
    * **Reservation Protocol:** Booking is available via telephone (0770-52-1733) with arrival required by 3:00 PM on the day of the activity. [Online email form](https://wakasa-hashi.com/contact) submissions are accepted up to 2 days prior to arrival.
* **Hours & Admission:**
    * **Admission Fee:** Free.
    * **Operating Hours:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM on weekdays, Sundays, and public holidays.
    * **Regular Closures:** Thursday *(Postponed to Friday if Thursday matches a public holiday)* and the New Year holiday period.
* **Official Website:** https://wakasa-hashi.com/

## 💡 Author’s Tips

* **Required Time:** Visitors should allocate slightly over 1 hour to fully complete the facility tour and the hands-on polishing workshop at the central museum.
* **Logistical Strategy:** Because Obama City is located in a specialized geographic region, round-trip travel times are considerable. Visitors traveling from major metropolitan areas like Tokyo or Osaka should plan a full 1-day itinerary to ensure sufficient transit buffers. 
* **Local Mobility:** A 1-day schedule allows extra time to utilize local taxi services or rental bicycles to explore the historical town area surrounding Obama Station.
