## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Geographical Position:** Located in the southern region of Niigata Prefecture, sharing an administrative border with Nagano Prefecture.
* **Spatial Dimensions:** Total land area encompasses 590.39 square kilometers.
* **Topographical Features:** The Shinano River, recorded as the longest river corridor in Japan, flows along a north-south axis through the center, creating a prominent river terrace structure.
* **Transit Distance:** Located approximately 2 hours from Tokyo Station via multi-modal rail transfers.

---

## 💡 Author's Recommendations

* **Temporal Frame:** The optimal exploration schedule ranges from 0.5 days to a 2-day, 1-night stay.
* **Accommodation & Wellness Assets:** The municipality contains numerous thermal hot spring facilities. Lodging configurations include traditional Japanese inns (Ryokan), western-style hotels, and specialized experiential art installations designed for overnight stays.
* **Logistical Strategy:** To effectively navigate the geographically dispersed assets and experience the regional heritage, it is highly recommended to plan for an overnight itinerary paired with a rental vehicle managed from the central transit station.

---

## 🗺️ Access

* **Primary Transit Route:** Tokyo Station → JR Joetsu Shinkansen (approx. 1 hour 20 minutes) → Echigo-Yuzawa Station → Hokuetsu Express Hokuhoku Line (approx. 40 minutes) → Tokamachi Station or Matsudai Station.
* **Transit Recommendation:** Visitors arriving via the rail network are advised to secure a rental vehicle directly at the destination station to optimize regional mobility.

## 🌾 Landform & Cultural Landmarks

### ⛰️ Hoshitoge Terraced Rice Fields
![Hoshitoge Terraced Rice Fields](/images/prefectures/niigata/hoshitoge_rice_field.webp)
* **Structural Profile:** Features approximately 200 operational rice paddies constructed across hillside slopes. It received official certification under the "Connecting Terraced Rice Fields Heritage" initiative by the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) in 2022.
* **Hydrological Phases:** During the spring snowmelt period (prior to planting) and late autumn (late October until permanent snowfall), the paddies fill with water, forming a high-reflectivity surface known as a "Water Mirror."
* **Access Routes:**
    * **Rail & Taxi:** 20 minutes via taxi from Matsudai Station on the Hokuhoku Line.
    * **Automobile:** 1 hour from Echigo-Kawaguchi IC (Kan-etsu Expressway) via Routes 117 and 253; 1 hour from Shiozawa-Ishiuchi IC via Route 353; or 1 hour from Joetsu IC (Hokuriku Expressway) via Route 253.

### 🏞️ Kiyotsu Gorge / Tunnel of Light
![Kiyotsu Gorge](/images/prefectures/niigata/kiyotsu_gorge.webp)
* **Geological Status:** Categorized as one of the three major canyons in Japan, featuring steep V-shaped volcanic rock walls with prominent columnar jointing formations along the Kiyotsu River. Designated as a National Scenic Spot and Natural Monument.
* **Architectural Intervention:** Renovated in 2018 by MAD Architects (led by Ma Yansong) for the Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale. The artistic restructuring incorporates the five natural elements (wood, earth, metal, fire, water) into the historic tunnel space.
* **Facility Extensions:** The entrance terminal operates a foot bath (closed during the winter season), a retail shop, and a cafe corridor.
* **Operational Rules:**
    * **Reservation System:** High-congestion dates operate under a strict mandatory advance-booking reservation framework.
    * **Pricing Structure:** Operates on a seasonal rate system divided into High Season (April 21 – November 20) and Off-Season (November 21 – April 20).
* **Access Routes:**
    * **Automobile:** 25 minutes from Shiozawa-Ishiuchi IC via Route 353.
    * **Bus & Foot:** 25 minutes via express bus from Echigo-Yuzawa Station (bound for Morimiyanohara) or 20 minutes via bus from Echigo-Tazawa Station, followed by a 30-minute walk from the "Kiyotsukyo Iruguchi" bus stop.
* **Official Website:** https://nakasato-kiyotsu.com/

### 🏺 National Treasure Flame-Style Vessel (Tokamachi City Museum)
![Tokamachi City Museum](/images/prefectures/niigata/tokamachi_city_museum.webp)
* **Artifact Profile:** Crafted approximately 5,000 years ago during the Mid-Jomon period, defined by large upper protrusions resembling stylized fire structures.
* **Legal Classifications:** Excavated from the Sasayama site, the collective inventory of pottery and stone tools was designated a National Treasure in June 1999 under the name "Deep Pottery Jars Excavated from the Sasayama Site in Niigata Prefecture." The designation comprises 57 primary objects (plus 871 attached pieces), including 14 distinct flame-style vessels. This marked the first Jomon pottery asset to receive National Treasure status in Japan.
* **Exhibition Status:** Maintained on permanent display inside the Tokamachi City Museum.
* **Access Route:** A 10-minute walk from Tokamachi Station (West Exit).

### 🌸 Gimyo Terraced Rice Fields
![Gimyo Terraced Rice Fields](/images/prefectures/niigata/gimyo_rice_field.webp)
* **Visual Characteristics:** Notable for wild mountain cherry trees (Yamazakura) that reflect off the water surfaces during peak bloom, which typically occurs from late April to the early May holiday period. The location meets seasonal cross-points for snow-cherry overlays in spring and autumn-snow transitions. Certified as a MAFF "Connecting Terraced Rice Fields Heritage" asset in 2022.
* **Location Caution:** Situated directly adjacent to Route 253; visitors must use the designated viewing platform and avoid parking on the public roadway.
* **Access Routes:**
    * **Rail & Taxi:** 8 minutes via taxi from Matsudai Station.
    * **Automobile Navigation:** Set coordinates to Gamo 2108-1, Tokamachi City (Yamadaira Post Office), then proceed 500 meters toward Joetsu.

## 🍽️ Gourmet
![Hegi Soba](/images/prefectures/niigata/hegi_soba.webp)
### 🥢 Kojimaya Sohonten (Recommended Restaurant)
* **Operational History:** Established in 1922, operating a continuous 100-year traditional production technique utilizing house-milled buckwheat ground via stone mills.
* **Product Specifications:** Specializes in "Hegi Soba," a noodle style bound with Funori (seaweed) instead of wheat. Invented by the first-generation artisan Jutaro using local textile techniques as structural inspiration, resulting in a smooth texture.
* **Design Features:** The entrance architecture houses the "Hegi Soba Historical Picture Scroll" paired with interior earthen walls crafted by master plasterer Kenichi Nagumo.
* **Schedule & Access:**
    * **Operating Hours:** 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Last Order). Subject to temporary irregular closures.
    * **Access Route:** Located 5 kilometers (a 10-minute drive) from Tokamachi Station. Alternatively accessible within 30 minutes from Muikamachi IC or 20 minutes from Echigo-Kawaguchi IC.

### 🍙 Kome no Eki
* **Operational Profile:** A specialized takeout retail shop specializing in Japanese rice balls (Onigiri) made from locally cultivated Uonuma Koshikari rice. It acts as the closest Onigiri retail outlet to Kiyotsu Gorge. Freshly harvested local agricultural vegetables are sold on-site.
* **Schedule & Access:**
    * **Operating Hours:** 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM.
    * **Regular Closures:** Wednesdays and Thursdays.
    * **Access Route:** A 20-minute drive from Kiyotsu Gorge, or a 45-minute drive from Shiozawa-Ishiuchi IC.

## ⚠️ Notes & Regulatory Codes for Agricultural Site Visitors

> ### 🛑 Mandatory Terraced Field Codes of Conduct
> Terraced rice fields are privately owned, working agricultural properties passed down through generational farming operations, not dedicated public tourist spaces. Visitors must strictly adhere to the following regulatory codes:
> * **Waste Management:** All personal refuse and garbage must be carried out of the site completely.
> * **Property Boundaries:** Entering active paddies, ridge paths (Aze), or adjacent dry fields is strictly prohibited. Camera tripods must not be erected inside private agricultural property lines to avoid illegal trespassing violations.
> * **Foraging Restriction:** Foraging for wild vegetables, cultivated crops, mushrooms, or mountain flora is prohibited regardless of whether the plants are wild or intentionally cultivated.
> * **Traffic Priority:** Agricultural machinery and utility vehicles hold absolute right-of-way on all access paths. Visitors must not obstruct farming operations.
> * **Portrait Rights:** Photographing agricultural workers without explicit prior consent is prohibited under portrait rights protections.
> * **Sanitation:** Public restroom facilities are non-existent within standard terraced fields. Visitors must utilize sanitation infrastructure at roadside stations (Michi-no-Eki) prior to site arrival.
> * **Night Operations:** The areas lack municipal street lighting at night. Movement during dark hours requires high-output illumination tools; traveling without active lights is strictly prohibited.
> * **Winter Closures:** Due to high-volume snow accumulation and associated safety risks, viewing and photography are completely restricted during the winter snowfall period.
