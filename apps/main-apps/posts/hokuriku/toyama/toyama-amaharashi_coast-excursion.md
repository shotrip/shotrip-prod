## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Geographical and Visual Features:** A coastal scenic zone located along Toyama Bay, characterized by sand beaches, pine trees, and a reef network. It offers an unobstructed maritime view of the 3,000-meter-class Tateyama Mountain Range over the water.
* **Cultural and Historical Background:** Designated as a historic scenic site recorded in Manyoshu poetry. The 8th-century poet Otomo no Yakamochi composed numerous pieces referencing this specific coastline.
* **Toponymic Origin:** Features the "Yoshitsune Rock" (Yoshitsune-iwa), where historical figure Minamoto no Yoshitsune reportedly sought shelter from a sudden rainstorm while fleeing to the Oshu region. This event serves as the administrative origin of the place name "Amaharashi" (meaning "rain clearing").
* **Official Environmental Certifications:** Officially registered under "Japan's Top 100 Beaches," "Top 100 White Sand and Green Pine Landscapes," and "Japan's 88 Best Bathing Beaches." The site functions as a shallow-water public swimming venue during the summer season.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

* **Required Time:** Visitors should allocate a minimum of 3 operational hours for the itinerary if combining the coastal viewing schedule with a dining stop at the regional sushi facility. 
* **Logistical Buffers:** Due to the low-density scheduling of local trains and bus networks, itineraries require structured time buffers to account for transit wait intervals.
* **Transit Modality Evaluation:** Setting Takaoka Station as a logistical base and deploying a rental vehicle ensures highly efficient mobility across the region. However, because the JR Himi Line itself provides premium scenic transit corridors directly along the coast, deploying public transit via a flexible, long-window schedule stands as the recommended methodology to fully observe the landscape.

## 🗺️ Access

### 🚆 Rail Routing
* **Primary Route:** JR Himi Line Amaharashi Station → Walk (5 minutes).
* **Transfer Protocol from Toyama Station:** Board the Ainokaze Toyama Railway to Takaoka Station, then transfer directly to the JR Himi Line.

### 🚌 Seasonal Bus Infrastructure
* **Operational Window:** The "Toyama Buri Kani Bus" specialized transit link operates seasonal routes servicing the area from October through March.

## 📸 Spatial Layout & Vantage Points

### 🪨 Onna-iwa Rock and the Tateyama Range
![Amaharashi Coast Onna-iwa Rock](/images/prefectures/toyama/amaharashi_onna_iwa.webp)
The geographic alignment framing the "Onna-iwa" (Female Rock) reef structure in the foreground with the background elevation of the Tateyama Mountain Range serves as the primary visual composition representing the location.

### 🚉 Himi Line Railway Intersections at Michi-no-Eki
The Michi-no-Eki Amaharashi roadside station features elevated public observation platforms that capture simultaneous lines of sight containing both the active JR Himi Line coastal tracks and the Tateyama Mountain Range.
* **Vantage Point Optimization:** While the facility operates public decks on both the second and third floors, the open-air third-floor deck provides the optimal, unobstructed field of view for tracking coastal rail traffic.

## 🍽️ Gourmet
![Sushi](/images/prefectures/toyama/takaoka_sushi.webp)
### 🍣 Kitokito Sushi (Himi Main Branch)
* **Operational History:** Opened in 1988 as the original flagship branch of the Kitokito Sushi conveyor-belt restaurant network across the Hokushinetsu region.
* **Ingredient Sourcing Protocols:**
    * **Marine Products:** Procured daily via direct bidding operations at the local Himi Wholesale Market to isolate seasonal regional species.
    * **Rice (Shari) Specifications:** Utilizes "Tentakaku," a certified regional brand rice, boiled using local well water assets.
    * **Sauce Components:** Deploys a natural fermentation vinegar to preserve freshness aroma across all branches, paired with a specialized proprietary soy sauce blend sourced from the Kanazawa-Ono brewing district.
* **Menu Configurations:** Features dynamic individual item pricing categorized under daily market plates ("Today's Special," "Manager's Selection," "Today's Himi Catch") alongside fixed-price weekday lunch sets configured with selectable soups and beverages.
* **Ordering Infrastructure:** Integrates digital touchscreen panels alongside traditional direct-vocal order requests to the onsite chefs.
* **Schedule & Access:**
    * **Operating Hours:** 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM.
    * **Regular Closures:** Alternating Tuesdays. *(Note: To align systematically with the scheduled closures of the Himi Wholesale Market, operations are temporarily shifted to every Wednesday until further notice).*
    * **Transit Route:** Amaharashi Station → Local Bus (Fushiki Station-Himi Route) → Yanagida Bus Stop → Walk (15 minutes).
