## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Geographical Proximity:** Located adjacent to Kanazawa City, accessible within a 15-to-30-minute transit via rail or automobile from Kanazawa Station.
* **Topographical Dimensions:** Encompasses Mount Hakusan, categorized as one of Japanese Three Holy Mountains alongside Mount Fuji and Mount Tateyama. The region features the Tedori Gorge rock formations and multiple mountain thermal springs.
* **Cultural Status:** Houses Shirayama Hime Shrine, which functions as the head shrine for approximately 3,000 Hakusan shrines distributed across Japan, tracking over 2,000 years of documented institutional history.

---

## 💡 Author’s Tips

* **Logistical Strategy:** The primary regional transit network relies on the Hokuriku Railroad Ishikawa Line originating from Nishi-Kanazawa Station. However, due to low daily train frequencies and minimal secondary public transit connections at individual stations, deploying a rental vehicle from Kanazawa serves as the optimal travel methodology. Standard tourism facilities across Hakusan City are equipped with dedicated parking infrastructure.
* **Temporal Allocation:** A single day (1-day itinerary) is the benchmark standard for completing the regional loop if utilizing Kanazawa as a starting base with an automobile.
* **Seasonal Operational Constraints:** The region registers some of the highest annual snowfall in Japan. Multiple structural landmarks, outdoor activities, and exhibition spaces suspend operations entirely during the winter period. Visitors must verify real-time status updates via official facility portals prior to departure.

## 🛕 Cultural & Geological Landmarks

### 🏠 Hakusanroku Folk Museum
![Hakusanroku Folk Museum](/images/prefectures/ishikawa/hakusanroku_folk_museum.webp)
* **Operational Profile:** An open-air historical facility that reconstructs the architecture and socio-technological history of residents from the Hakusan foothills. Original historic residences were systematically dismantled, relocated, and reassembled on-site to preserve period tools and mountain village structures.
* **Experiential Modules:** Conducts structured technical workshops for traditional straw weaving (Wara-zaiku) and regional Obaru vegetable dyeing.
* **Schedule & Entry:**
    * **Operating Hours:** 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM *(Final gate entry locks at 4:00 PM)*.
    * **Regular Closures:** Thursdays *(Postponed to Friday if Thursday is a public holiday)* and the designated winter season closure window.
    * **Fee Structure:** General Adults: 260 JPY / Seniors (Ages 65+): 200 JPY / Students (High School and below): Free. Holders of a same-day valid admission pass for the Hakusan Dinosaur Park Shiramine receive a discounted rate of 150 JPY.

### 🚡 Sky Shishiku (Shishiku Highlands)
![Sky Shishiku Paragliding](/images/prefectures/ishikawa/sky_shishiku_paragliding.webp)
* **Topographical Details:** Positioned at an elevation of 650 meters, accessible via an aerial gondola lift system from Park Shishiku.
* **Geopark Classification:** Serves as a primary observation node within the Hakusan Tedori River UNESCO Global Geopark, offering complete lines of sight across the Tedori River alluvial fan, the Kaga Plain, and the Sea of Japan.
* **Infrastructure Equipment:** Operates a summit cafe terminal serving hot beverages, juices, ice cream, and light meals, paired with an expansive dedicated dog run zone calibrated for small and medium-sized breeds.
* **Aviation Activities:** The geographic placement captures consistent marine wind patterns from the Sea of Japan, optimizing conditions for paragliding operations. The onsite flight school provides two-person tandem flights managed by certified instructors.
* **Schedule & Entry:**
    * **Operating Hours:** Standard: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM *(Gondola ascent halts at 4:30 PM)*. Extended Sunset Hours: Second Saturday of each month (May to October) operates 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM. High-Season Variant (July 25 to August 10): 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM *(Note: August 8 extends to 8:00 PM)*.
    * **Regular Closures:** Tuesdays and the winter snowy period.
    * **Gondola Fee:** Adults: 710 JPY round-trip (500 JPY one-way) / Elementary Students: 300 JPY round-trip (250 JPY one-way) / Pre-school Children: Free.
    * **Access Route:** 20 minutes south from Hakusan IC via Route 157.
* **Website:** https://city-hakusan.com/shishiku/

### 🛕 Shirayama Hime Shrine
![Shirayama Hime Shrine](/images/prefectures/ishikawa/shirayama_hime_shrine.webp)
* **Institutional Profile:** The absolute headquarters (Sohongu) of the nationwide Hakusan shrine network. The main deity enshrined is Shirayama Hime no Ookami, alternatively designated as Kukurihime no Kami, historically associated with the integration and binding of human relations (Kukuru).
* **Botanical Assets:** The primary front approach (Omotesando) and internal sacred grounds are bordered by ancient Cryptomeria (Sugi) and Thujopsis (Asunaro) trees with estimated ages reaching up to 1,000 years.
* **Monthly Rituals:** Conducts the traditional "Otsuitachi-mairi" pilgrimage on the 1st day of every calendar month.
* **Treasure House Archive:** Houses historical artifacts, religious icons, and officially designated Important Cultural Properties.
* **Schedule & Entry:**
    * **Treasure House Hours:** 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM *(November window shifts to 9:30 AM – 3:30 PM)*.
    * **Treasure House Closures:** Dec. 1 to Mar. 31.
    * **Treasure House Fee:** Adults: 300 JPY / Students (High School and below): Free.
    * **Access Route:** 30 minutes south from Hakusan IC via Route 157.
* **Official Website:** https://www.shirayama.or.jp/en/

### 🏞️ Tedori Gorge
![Tedori Gorge](/images/prefectures/ishikawa/tedori_gorge.webp)
* **Geological Dimensions:** A continuous vertical canyon carved by the Tedori River, extending 8 kilometers from the Komon Bridge in Kamashimizu-machi to the Taizan Bridge in Kawarayama-machi. The sheer rock cliffs maintain uniform heights ranging from 20 to 30 meters.
* **Primary Vantage Points:** Visual tracking is optimal from the Komon and Furo structural bridges.
* **Watagataki Waterfall:** A major hydrological feature within the gorge segment, where the water current drops vertically from a measured height of 32 meters.
* **Access Route:** 50 minutes south from Hakusan IC via Route 157.

### ♨️ Shiramine Onsen Soyu
* **Spatial Context:** Situated in the center of the Shiramine district, which holds official protection status as a National Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings.
* **Hydrological Details:** A 100% natural, unadulterated thermal spring source classified chemically as a sodium hydrogen carbonate spring. The high alkalinity provides an exceptionally smooth skin-softening profile, colloquially documented as "Kinu-hada no Yu" (Silk Skin Water).
* **Schedule & Entry:**
    * **Operating Hours:** Weekdays: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM / Weekends and Holidays: 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM *(Final reception desk check-in at 8:30 PM)*.
    * **Regular Closures:** Tuesdays *(Postponed to Wednesday if Tuesday matches a public holiday)* and the New Year holiday period.
    * **Fee:** Adults: 750 JPY / Elementary Students: 360 JPY / Children (Ages 3+): 260 JPY.
    * **Access Route:** 80 minutes south from Hakusan IC via Route 157.
* **Official Website:** https://www.shiramine-m.com/
