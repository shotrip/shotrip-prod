## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Location:** Imabari City, Ehime Prefecture, is the most crucial "hack point" in northern Shikoku, where you can experience the overwhelming scale and true potential of Japanese craftsmanship.
* **Craftsmanship:** This city is the absolute holy land of "Imabari Towel," chosen and purchased by luxury hotels worldwide, allowing you to encounter the history and skills of artisans everywhere you go.
* **Local Culture:** However, the real thrill of Imabari lies behind its sophisticated craftsmanship—its face as a genuinely wild port town.
* **Experience:** After hacking the dynamic stone walls of Imabari Castle, which directly draws in the fierce currents of the Seto Inland Sea, head to the Teppan Yakitori joints where locals gather every night. Imabari's cheap, fast, and unbelievably delicious B-grade street food will recharge a traveler’s energy to the absolute limit. Imabari is a powerful town where you can fully collect the core of unpretentious local culture and Japanese industrial history all in a single day.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🧸 Shimanami Wooden Toy Museum
* **Establishment:** This facility is the 15th toy museum built in Japan, supervised by the authorized NPO "Association of Art and Play Creativity," a certified NPO that also operates the Tokyo Toy Museum.
* **Purpose:** It functions as a regional connection point focused on "wood education" (Mokuiku), utilizing toys and play to introduce regional history, culture, and nature.
* **Official Website:** https://shimanami-wooden-toy-museum.com/

### 🎨 Towel Museum
![Imabari Towel Museum](/images/prefectures/ehime/imabari_towel_museum.webp)
* **Features:** Located in the Asakura region near Mount Kasamatsu within the Setonaikai National Park. The main building consists of four floors containing retail areas for towels and specialty goods from the Shikoku region, as well as restaurants and cafes.
* **Exhibits:** The paid gallery area includes demonstrations of the towel manufacturing process and various towel art installations. A 33,000-square-meter European-style garden is accessible on the grounds.
* **Official Website:** https://www.towelmuseum.com/

### 🏯 Imabari Castle
![Imabari Castle](/images/prefectures/ehime/imabari_castle.webp)
* **History:** The structure was designed and constructed by Todo Takatora during the early Edo period. It was originally a large flatland castle facing the sea, featuring a square design optimized for military defense and surrounded by a triple moat system.
* **Maritime Connection:** An internal harbor was built within the castle to connect directly with the ocean. This layout formed the historical foundation for the current Imabari Port.
* **Present Structures:** The inner moat and original central stone walls remain intact. The moat connects directly to the sea, allowing saltwater fish to live inside it. The main keep, turrets, and the Kurogane-gomon Gate have been reconstructed since 1980.
* **Exhibits & Features:** The interior of the keep houses a museum dedicated to local history, nature and arts. The top floor serves as a 360-degree observation deck overlooking the Shimanami Kaido, where visitors can use the "Imabari Castle AR" smartphone app to view the historical layout of the castle grounds.
* **Illumination:** The castle is lit nightly by approximately 100 lights from 30 minutes past sunset until 22:00.

### 🌉 Itoyama Park & Kurushima Kaikyo View Museum
![Itoyama Park](/images/prefectures/ehime/imabari_itoyama_park_view.webp)
* **Overview:** A scenic observation area located at the Imabari trailhead of the Shimanami Kaido. The site provides a direct view of the Kurushima Strait and the Kurushima Kaikyo Bridge.
* **Historical Site:** The Itoyama Observatory looks directly over Kurushima Island, which historically served as a base for the Kurushima Murakami clan at the entrance of Hatohama Bay.

### 🚢 Kurushima Kaikyo Tidal Current Cruise
![Tidal Current Cruise](/images/prefectures/ehime/imabari_tidal_current_cruise.webp)
* **Activity:** A 45-minute guided boat tour through the Kurushima Strait, which features one of Japanese three fastest tidal currents.
* **Route:** The vessel travels beneath the Kurushima Kaikyo Bridge, navigates around Kurushima, Oshima, and Umashima islands, and passes near the commercial shipyards in Hatohama.
* **Website:** https://imabari-shimanami.jp/kyuuryuu/

### 🐓 Toribayashi (Teppan Yakitori)
* **Overview:** An established eatery specializing in iron-plate (*teppan*) yakitori for approximately 40 years, currently managed by the founder's successor.
* **Menu Specialties:** The restaurant uses mature chicken (*oyadori*), characterized by its firm texture and deep flavor profile. Key items include crispy chicken skin (*kawa*) and *Senzanki*, a regional style of seasoned Japanese fried chicken.

### 🐟 Michi-no-Eki Yoshiumi Ikiiki-kan
* **Overview:** A roadside rest station equipped with a large free parking area, located adjacent to the Kurushima Kaikyo Bridge.
* **Services:** The facility houses a tourism information center, a regional souvenir store selling local produce and fresh fish, and a live seafood barbecue area utilizing charcoal grills. Deep-fried fish cakes (*Jakoten*) are made on-site during weekends.
* **Boat Tour:** The station also serves as a departure point for a 50-minute tidal current cruise. Departure times vary depending on the tide.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

A day trip (starting from Takamatsu, Matsuyama, or Okayama) is recommended. Since the sightseeing spots are scattered and public transportation like buses is not very strong, it is better to consider renting a car as essential. There are also many ryokan and accommodations, so if you stay overnight for 2 days and 1 night, it is also a good option to skip renting a car and take a leisurely stroll according to the schedule of public transportation. It is a highly exciting tourist destination with so many highlights that it takes from morning until night if you try to cover everything.
