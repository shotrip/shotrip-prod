## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Agricultural Production:** Konan City ranks first in Japan for the production volume of chives and first within Kochi Prefecture for the production volume of mandarin oranges.
* **Key Facilities:** The municipality houses regional infrastructure including the Noichi Zoological Park (ranked first in the zoo and aquarium category on a major travel review site for two consecutive years), the Tsukimiyama Kodomo-no-Mori athletic facility, and coastal rest areas facing the Pacific Ocean.
* **Cultural Assets:** The area preserves historical assets related to the artist Kinzo (commonly known as Ekin), including traditional painted screens displayed during the local Ekin Festival, the Tei Bon Odori which has been observed for over 400 years, and the Dorome Festival featuring regional sake and fresh fish crops.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🎨 Ekin-gura Museum
![Ekin-gura Museum](/images/prefectures/kochi/konan_ekingura.webp)
* **Historical Context:** Originally an artist of the Kano school serving the Tosa domain's chief retainer (the Kirima family), the painter Kinzo (Ekin) was exiled from the castle town following a forgery incident. He subsequently settled in the town of Akaoka and utilized a local sake brewery as his studio.
* **Exhibits:** The museum permanently houses, preserves, and displays 23 historical festival painted screens (*shibaie-byobu*) that remain within the local municipality.

### 🎭 Bentenza Theater
* **Structure & Purpose:** A traditional Japanese theater structure equipped with functional stage mechanisms, including a revolving stage (*mawari-butai*), a walkway through the audience (*hanamichi*), and box seating (*masu-seki*). The venue serves as a cultural exchange center and holds regular public performances, including Ekin Kabuki.
* **Official Website:** https://bentenza.com/kochi-kabuki-experience/

### 🏖️ Yasea Park
![Yasea Park](/images/prefectures/kochi/konan_ya_sea_park.webp)
* **Overview:** Yasea Park is a scenic, palm-fringed seaside park in Konan City, Kochi. It features a beautiful sandy beach, picnic areas, and local food markets, making it a perfect spot for relaxation and recreation.
* **Annual Events Schedule:** This coastal facility hosts numerous regional events throughout the year, including:
  * **Mid-July:** Marine Festival YASU
  * **August 15:** Konan City Tei Bon Odori
  * **Late November:** Autumn Festival
  * **December:** Coastal Illumination displays
* **Recreational Use:** The park serves as a public venue for seasonal swimming and marine sports activities.
* **Official Website:** https://yasea.jp/

## 💡 Author’s Tips

If you are basing your trip out of Kochi City, a half-day is enough. On the other hand, if you are starting from Takamatsu or Okayama, a day trip is also perfectly feasible. If you are traveling from any further away than that, it would be better to relocate your base of operations closer. It is a town that faces the Pacific Ocean and is exceptionally peaceful, so it is best to come visit with the goal of slow-living and taking your time. Although the Gomen-Nahari Line running through the city does not have a high frequency of trains, the stunning panoramic view of the Pacific Ocean from the train windows is highly worth seeing. There are even tourists who come specifically just to ride this local line.
