## 🏛️ Introduction

* **History:** Takamatsu Castle was completed in 1590 by Ikoma Chikamasa. Later, the Matsudaira clan ruled the castle for 228 years.
* **Main Keep:** The original main castle tower was completed in 1669. It was the largest in Shikoku, but was taken down in 1884 due to old age.
* **Current Status:** The site opened as Takamatsu Municipal Tamamo Park in 1955 and was selected as one of the "Top 100 Japanese Castles" in 2006. The Sakura-gomon Gate was rebuilt in 2022.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🚪 Sakura-gomon Gate
![Sakura-gomon Gate](/images/prefectures/kagawa/takamatsu_castle_sakura_gomon.webp)
* **History:** It is shown in an early Edo period screen painting of Takamatsu Castle town, meaning it was built early.
* **Reconstruction:** It was selected to become a National Treasure in 1944, but burned down in the Takamatsu air raid. Reconstruction was finished in 2022.

### 🏛️ Hiunkaku
![Takamatsu Castle Hiunkaku](/images/prefectures/kagawa/takamatsu_castle_hiunkaku.webp)
* **History:** It was built in 1917 as a villa for the Matsudaira family. It was used as a guest house, and Emperor Showa once stayed here.
* **Status:** It is a National Important Cultural Property built in the traditional Shoin-zukuri style. Today, Takamatsu City owns it, and citizens use it for tea parties and concerts.

### 🌿 Hiunkaku Garden
![Takamatsu Castle Hiunkaku Garden](/images/prefectures/kagawa/takamatsu_castle_hiunkaku_garden.webp)
* **History:** This garden was built in the Taisho period together with Hiunkaku, and parts of it date back to the Edo period.
* **Style:** It is a Karesansui (dry landscape) garden that uses rocks and sand to show mountains and water without real water.
* **Features:** It has a 150-meter curved dry riverbed and a stone bridge made of Aji stone, a high-quality stone from eastern Takamatsu.

### 🌉 Saya-bashi Bridge
![Takamatsu Castle Saya-bashi](/images/prefectures/kagawa/takamatsu_castle_sayabashi.webp)
* **Function:** The Honmaru (inner bailey) sits in the middle of the inner moat, and visitors can only reach it by crossing this bridge. The bridge was designed to be destroyed to protect the Honmaru during an emergency.
* **Design:** When first built, it had no roof and was called "Rankan-bashi." A roof and side walls were added during Edo period repairs, making it look like a sword scabbard (saya).

### 🏯 Honmaru
* **Features:** The main castle tower base stands inside the Honmaru across the Saya-bashi Bridge. It has a viewing deck at the highest spot in the park.
* **View:** Visitors can see the relationship between the city area, the Seto Inland Sea, and Takamatsu Station. The moat water is entirely seawater.

### 🐟 Taigan Joju (Fish Feeding)
![Fish Feeding](/images/prefectures/kagawa/takamatsu_castle_fish_feeding.webp)
* **Moat Water:** Seawater is pulled from the Seto Inland Sea, so many sea fish live in the moat, such as sea bass, grey mullet, and farmed red sea bream.
* **Activity:** Visitors can do a fish-feeding activity named "Taigan Joju" (a play on words for "wishes coming true"). Throwing food to the fish is said to make wishes come true. There are 2 feeding spots.
* **Tide Conditions:** The water level changes with the tide. At high tide, fish gather near the water gate that links the sea and the moat. At low tide, the best spot is near the "Horibata" edge of the Sannomaru. Food is sold in vending machines.

### 🚢 Castle Boat Experience
![Castle Boat Experience](/images/prefectures/kagawa/takamatsu_castle_boat_experience.webp)
* **Activity:** Visitors can ride a tour boat named "Tamamomaru," which is a reproduction of an Edo-period boat, guided by a boatman. It runs 9 times daily from near the water gate to the keep base.
* **Castle View:** The main feature is looking up at the castle keep base from below. The stone wall comes very close to the boat.
* **Wall Structure:** The stones at the corners are stacked alternately using long rectangular pieces. This method is called Sangi-zumi, which increases wall strength.
* **Fans:** The fans provided on the boat show the old main keep. Holding it up toward the base shows the original size of the keep. Fish gather around the boat, so visitors can feed them during the ride.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

Duration of stay here is 1 to 2 hours. If you stroll around Takamatsu Castle slowly and also try the castle boat experience, it will take about this much time. The strength of this place is its excellent access, as you can easily drop by on foot right after arriving at Takamatsu Station and dropping off your luggage. For people using Takamatsu, the gateway to Shikoku, as a base, you can visit just like taking a walk. It is also an easy place to access by train across the Seto Ohashi Bridge from Okayama on the main island, so people based in Okayama can also come for a casual sightseeing trip. I often stop by here when taking a leisurely stroll after filling my stomach with udon.
