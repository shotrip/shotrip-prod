## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Geographical and Commercial Status:** Located in Shizuoka City, Shizuoka Prefecture, Shimizu Port serves as a major base for deep-sea fishing and records the highest volume of frozen tuna landings in Japan.
* **Proximity to Urban Centers:** Positioned approximately 1 hour from central Tokyo via the Tokaido Shinkansen and local rail connections, providing accessible seafood markets such as "Kashi-no-Ichi" near the station area.
* **Cultural Assets:** Features Miho-no-Matsubara, a coastal pine grove designated as part of a UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site, offering lines of sight to Mount Fuji across Suruga Bay.

---

## 💡 Author’s Tips

* **Temporal Allocation:** A single day (1-day day-trip itinerary) is the recommended standard for exploring this region. The area functions effectively as a single-day stopover destination to experience the Pacific coastline and regional culture.
* **Transit Modality:** Operating a rental car is highly restricted for this itinerary due to the prominent drinking cultures associated with local seafood dining and the evening oden stalls. 
* **Public Transportation Advantages:** The district features highly developed public bus, rail, and taxi systems. Visitors can execute the complete sightseeing loop using trains, buses, and walking routes without a private vehicle.

## 🌲 Cultural & Historical Landmarks

### 🚢 Shimizu Port (Shimizu Marine Terminal)
![Shimizu Port](/images/prefectures/shizuoka/shimizu_port.webp)
* **Chronological History:** Documented maritime usage dates back to the 600s (Nara period) as a departure point for military ships to Baekje. From the 1500s to the 1700s, the Tokugawa Shogunate used the port as a strategic military outpost and trade gateway.
* **Modern Development:** Designated an official international trade port on August 4, 1899. Initial modern operations centered on direct green tea exports, expanding later to citrus fruits, canned goods, motorcycles, and musical instruments.
* **Industrial Support:** Implemented early container transport infrastructure during the rapid economic growth period, handling imports of raw materials such as timber, soybeans, and bauxite to support the regional economy.
* **Access:** JR Shizuoka Station → Tokaido Line → JR Shimizu Station → Local Bus (Miho Yamanote Line) → Manseicho → Walk (6 minutes).

### 🏞️ Miho-no-Matsubara
![Miho-no-Matsubara](/images/prefectures/shizuoka/miho_no_matsubara.webp)
* **Physical Dimensions:** A scenic coastal area spanning 7 kilometers along the shore, populated by approximately 30,000 pine trees. 
* **Heritage Classification:** Designated as a component of the Mount Fuji UNESCO World Cultural Heritage site in 2013 due to its historical and artistic relationship with the mountain.
* **Mihoshirube (Culture Center):** Opened in March 2019, this museum provides multi-language educational exhibits detailing the history and cultural value of the pine grove.
    * **Hours & Admission:** Open 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM daily. Admission is free.
* **Access:** JR Shizuoka Station → Tokaido Line → JR Shimizu Station → Local Bus (Miho Yamanote Line) → Higashi-orido → Walk (13 minutes).
* **Official Website:** https://miho-no-matsubara.jp/lang/en/

### 🌅 Nihondaira Yume Terrace
![Nihondaira Yume Terrace](/images/prefectures/shizuoka/nihondaira_yume_terrace.webp)
* **Structural Architecture:** An observation facility designed by Kengo Kuma & Associates, featuring extensive structural wood components.
* **Topographical Vantage:** Situated on a 300-meter mountain, featuring a 200-meter outdoor corridor that provides a 360-degree panoramic view of Suruga Bay, Mount Fuji, Shimizu Port, and Miho-no-Matsubara.
* **Hours & Admission:** Open 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Saturdays and Sundays until 9:00 PM). Closed on the second Tuesday of each month and December 26–31. Admission is free.
* **Access Routes:** 
    * JR Shizuoka Station → Tokaido Line → JR Higashi-Shizuoka Station → Local Bus (Nihondaira Ropeway Line) → Nihondaira Yume Terrace Entrance → Walk (4 minutes).
    * From Shimizu Marine Terminal → Walk (16 minutes) → Manseicho → Local Bus (Yamahara Baingenji Line) → Kunozanshita → Kunozan Station → Nihondaira Ropeway → Nihondaira Station → Walk (3 minutes).

### 🛕 Kunozan Toshogu Shrine
![Kunozan Toshogu Shrine](/images/prefectures/shizuoka/kunozan_toshogu_shrine.webp)
* **Institutional Profile:** Established in 1617 to enshrine the deified spirit of Tokugawa Ieyasu. The main sanctuary structure was designated a National Treasure in 2010.
* **Access Modes:** Connected to Nihondaira Yume Terrace via a 5-minute link on the Nihondaira Ropeway. Alternatively, visitors can ascend via a historic front approach consisting of 1,159 stone steps from the mountain base.
* **Museum Collection:** The summit museum archives over 2,000 historical items, including the personal daily artifacts of Tokugawa Ieyasu and arms/armor belonging to successive Tokugawa Shoguns.
* **Hours & Admission:** Open 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM daily.
* **Access Routes:** 
    * JR Shizuoka Station → Local Bus (Miwa Ootani Line) → Higashi-Ootani → Local Bus (Ishida Kaido Line) → Kuno-kyoku-mae → Walk (3 minutes).
    * From Shimizu Marine Terminal → Walk (16 minutes) → Manseicho → Local Bus (Yamahara Baingenji Line) → Kunozanshita → Walk (5 minutes).

### 🏯 Sunpu Castle Park
![Sunpu Castle Park](/images/prefectures/shizuoka/sunpu_castle.webp)
* **Historical Origins:** Located on the structural remnants of Sunpu Castle, built by Tokugawa Ieyasu where he spent his later years. Double moats and original stone walls remain intact.
* **Public Access:** Functions as a public municipal park. Paid admission entries are required to view the reconstructed Higashi-gomon Gate, Tatsumi-yagura Turret, and Momijiyama Garden.
* **Hours & Admission:** Park grounds are open for walking 24 hours (Ticketed interior historical facilities operate 9:00 AM – 4:30 PM). Paid structures close on Mondays.

### 🎨 Shizuoka City Tokaido Hiroshige Museum
![Tokaido Hiroshige Museum](/images/prefectures/shizuoka/tokaido_hiroshige_museum.webp)
* **Museum Profile:** The first museum in Japan dedicated to the Edo-period Ukiyo-e artist Utagawa Hiroshige. It is located inside Yui Honjin Park, the former site of the Yui-shuku post station along the Tokaido road.
* **Activities & Exhibits:** Displays alternating rotations of Hiroshige's artwork alongside related woodblock print exhibitions. Features a hands-on printing station in the entrance hall for a 300 JPY fee.
* **Hours & Admission:** Open 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (Final entrance at 4:30 PM). Closed on Mondays *(Postponed on Tuesday if Monday matches a public holiday).
* **Fee:** Adults: 520 Yen/ Univ or High School Students: 310 Yen/ Junior High or Elementary School Students: 130 Yen/ Pre-School: Free
* **Access:** Walk 20 minutes from JR Yui Station.

## 🍣 Gourmet Centers
![Aoba Oden Yokocho](/images/prefectures/shizuoka/aoba_oden_yokocho.webp)

### 🍣 Shimizu Sushi Yokocho
* **Facility Layout:** Located inside the Shimizu S-Pulse Dream Plaza commercial complex, housing 8 independent sushi restaurants arranged to mimic an old Edo-period downtown street layout.
* **Menu Options:** Restaurants provide visual photo menus featuring regional seafood sets paired with local Shizuoka sake and beers.
* **Hours:** Open 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily. (Last Order at 8:00 PM)
* **Access:** JR Shizuoka Station → Tokaido Line → JR Shimizu Station → Local Bus (Miho Yamanote Line) → Manseicho → Walk (6 minutes).
* **Official Website:** https://www.dream-plaza.co.jp/shimizu-sushi-yokocho/

### 🍦 Nanaya Shizuoka Store
* **Product Line:** Specializes in Shizuoka matcha gelato featuring seven progressive levels of flavor intensity, developed by Marushichi Seicha, a wholesale tea merchant operating since 1907.
* **Flavor:** Levels No. 1 through No. 6 cost 340 JPY each. Level No. 7 marks the highest concentration tier available. Level No. 4 represents the standardized benchmark balance of bitterness and sweetness.
* **Hours:** Open 11:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Closed on Wednesdays.
* **Access:** Walk 10 minutes from JR Shizuoka Station.

### 🍢 Aoba Yokocho Oden Alley
* **Socio-Cultural Context:** Replaces the historic Aoba Park street corridor, which originally hosted over 200 outdoor oden food carts in the past. The location functions as a concentrated indoor alley housing multiple independent stalls serving regional Shizuoka Oden.
* **Hours:** Depending on restaurants inside the street.
* **Access:** Walk 12 minutes from JR Shizuoka Station.
