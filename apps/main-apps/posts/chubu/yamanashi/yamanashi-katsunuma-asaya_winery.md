## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Corporate Foundations:** Asaya Winery (Asaya Vin Co., Ltd.) was established in 1921 in the Katsunuma region of Koshu City, Yamanashi Prefecture. 
* **Operational Philosophy:** The facility focuses on viticulture and vinification using locally harvested grapes to create wines that reflect the specific regional climate and soil conditions of Katsunuma.
* **Geographic Placement:** Situated in Katsunuma, located approximately 1.5 hours from central Tokyo, positioned near local dining and hot spring facilities.

---

## 💡 Author’s Tips

* **Required Time:** Visitors participating in the scheduled guided winery tour should allocate a time buffer of slightly under 2 hours. For itineraries limited exclusively to product purchases and tasting room sessions, a 1-hour window is sufficient.
* **Regional Exploration:** Because Koshu City is a prominent wine-producing district, visitors can utilize local taxi services to execute a multi-winery tour incorporating adjacent vineyards.
* **Transit Modality Evaluation:** Due to the availability of wine tastings, operating a personal vehicle is not recommended for this itinerary. The destination features efficient connectivity via the rail and taxi network, making public transport the optimal choice.

---

## 🗺️ Access

* **Primary Station:** JR Chuo Main Line Katsunuma-budogou Station → Taxi transfer (approx. 8 minutes).
* **Express Rail Options:** Select Limited Express "Azusa" trains operating from JR Shinjuku Station make scheduled stops directly at Katsunuma-budogou Station.

## 🍇 Cultural Landmarks & Guest Facilities

### 🛒 Direct-Sales Winery Shop & Tasting Room
* **Facility Layout:** The visitor entrance is positioned along a narrow lane at the rear of the winery structure. The interior space functions as a retail store and an active wine-tasting room.
* **Historical Exhibits:** The shop displays original vintage winemaking equipment, historical tools, and archival bottle labels tracking the company's early eras.
* **Product Inventory:** Stocks specialized direct-retail items, including traditional large 1.8-liter bottle wines (Isshobin wine) and 100% pure local grape juices.
* **Shipping Services:** Provides onsite processing for domestic home delivery and gift shipping services via commercial courier logistics.
* **Hours & Schedule:**
    * **Operating Hours:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM on weekdays *(Closes at 4:30 PM on Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays)*.
    * **Calendar Closures:** Open daily throughout the year, excluding the New Year holiday period. Temporary irregular closures are published via the official website news section.

### 🍷 Guided Winery Tour Program
* **Operational Detail:** A structured, guide-led walking tour through the production plant detailing the chronological steps of the wine manufacturing process, followed by a tasting session in the dedicated tasting room.
* **Schedules & Frequency:**
    * **Daily Time Slots:** Conducted twice daily at ① 11:30 AM and ② 2:00 PM.
    * **Duration:** Approximately 30 to 45 minutes per session.
    * **Calendar Availability:** Operational daily, excluding the New Year holiday period. Irregular closures are published via [the official Facebook portal](https://www.facebook.com/asayawine).
* **Booking & Registration:**
    * **Reservation Protocol:** Requires mandatory telephone booking at least 7 days prior to the intended visit date.
    * **Fees:** 500 JPY per participant (Junior High School students and older). Fees must be paid at the winery shop counter prior to the tour start time.
    * **Age Exemptions:** Students of High School age and below enter free of charge when accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Non-participating companions utilizing only the shop facility are exempt from fees.
    * **Official Website:** https://www.asaya-winery.jp/winery.html
