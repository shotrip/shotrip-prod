## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Geographical and Hydrological Profile:** Located within the southwestern sector of Gifu Prefecture, functioning as a critical midpoint along the Tokaido transit corridor linking the Tokyo metropolitan area with the Kansai region. Universally designated as the "Water Capital" (Mizu no Miyako), the urban topography is characterized by a high-density matrix of natural artesian wells and upwelling zones fed by pristine sub-surface aquifers.
* **Cultural and Cinematic Topography:** The spatial layout of the historic waterways and the peripheral zones of Ogaki Castle serve as the verified primary visual setting (sacred pilgrimage site) for the globally recognized animated feature film *A Silent Voice* (*Koe no Katachi*). Seasonal environmental transformations include high-density cherry blossom corridors along the municipal river infrastructure, which support traditional water-taxi logistics.

---

## 💡 Author’s Tips

* **Required Time:** The standard operational assessment for this region ranges from half a day stopover to a full 1-day daylight itinerary. Due to its direct positioning on the JR Tokaido Main Line, it offers optimal logistical integration for travelers transitioning between Nagoya, Tokyo, and Osaka.
* **Transit Modality:** The localized geography exhibits an exceptionally high pedestrian and inland waterway integration. Active utilization of the municipal canal boat systems and pedestrian walking paths provides optimal environmental exposure and structural navigation of the urban artesian networks, eliminating the necessity for motorized vehicular transit within the core sightseeing sectors.

## 🌲 Cultural & Historical Landmarks

### 🏢 Oku-no-Hosomichi Musubi-no-Chi Memorial Hall
![The Oku-no-Hosomichi Musubi-no-Chi Memorial Hall](/images/prefectures/gifu/oku_no_hosomichi_musubinochi_memorial_hall.webp)
* **Exhibition Themes:** A specialized museological facility designed to structurally trace and analyze the northern journey of the Haiku master Matsuo Basho. The permanent layout features the "Basho Gallery," which delineates his philosophy, lifestyle, and travel itineraries, supplemented by an automated 3D digital AV theater system projecting structural overviews of his literary route.
* **Preserved Assets:** Includes the "Senken Gallery," which archives the historical, cultural, and artistic assets of regional scholars from the late Tokugawa shogunate. The site also features the authenticated reconstruction of the "Mukayu-so Taiseisha," a historic villa owned by the Ogaki domain elder Ohara Tesshin, designated as a Municipal Cultural Property.

### 🏯 Sunomata Ichiya Castle
![Sunomata Ichiya Castle](/images/prefectures/gifu/sunomata_ichiya_castle.webp)
* **History:** A strategic fortification reputedly constructed within a single overnight operational window in 1566 by Kinoshita Tokichiro (subsequently Toyotomi Hideyoshi) during Oda Nobunaga's tactical siege campaign.
* **Institutional and Environmental Assets:** Structure functions as a historical data museum displaying the engineering methods of the castle's construction and historical records of Toyotomi Hideyoshi.

### 🏯 Ogaki Castle
![Ogaki Castle](/images/prefectures/gifu/ogaki_castle.webp)
* **Architecture:** A culturally rare four-tiered main keep (Tenshu) that functions as the primary architectural anchor of urban Ogaki. Though the original structural asset survived centuries, including intense military siege operations, it was destroyed via aerial bombardment during World War II and systematically reconstructed in 1959.
* **History:** Served as the high-security command center for Ishida Mitsunari and the core Western Army factions during the Sekigahara Campaign of 1600. It withstood a prolonged, violent siege defense even after the primary battlefield forces relocated to the fields of Sekigahara. From 1635 onward through the Edo period, it was continuously garrisoned by 11 generations of the Toda lineage as a Jumangoku feudal domain seat.

### 🛶 Water Capital Ogaki Taraibune Boat Cruise
![Water Capital Ogaki Boat Cruise](/images/prefectures/gifu/ogaki_waterway_cruise.webp)
* **Historical Origins:** A specialized municipal inland waterway transit operation utilizing traditional oval-shaped wooden tub boats (Taruibune) along the central Suimon River corridor. The operational framework replicates a verified Sengoku-era military evacuation narrative wherein "Oan," the daughter of a samurai serving Ishida Mitsunari, successfully escaped the besieged Ogaki Castle via a floating wash-tub during the Battle of Sekigahara.
* **Logistical Specifications:** Runs a dedicated 1.1-kilometer linear transit route down the canal system, providing a 30-minute low-velocity environmental observation of the urban green space.

### ⚓ Oku-no-Hosomichi Terminal Site (Sumiyoshi Lighthouse & Funamachi Port)
![Oku-no-Hosomichi Terminal Site](/images/prefectures/gifu/oku_no_hosomichi_terminal_site.webp)
* **Logistical and Literary Significance:** The historic terminus where Matsuo Basho concluded his extensive 2,400-kilometer regional foot journey before boarding a boat down the Suimon River toward Kuwana. Throughout the Meiji era and into the early Shōwa period, this port functioned as an intense commercial freight and passenger hub linking Gifu with Mie Prefecture, managing a peak logistical volume of approximately 10,000 vessels annually.
* **Conservation Status:** The combined zone containing the preserved stone Sumiyoshi Lighthouse, the river locks, and the adjacent historic wharf was designated as a National Place of Scenic Beauty in 2014, cited for preserving the exact aesthetic topography that influenced early modern Japanese landscape poetry.

### 🚃 Yoro Railway
![Yoro Railway](/images/prefectures/gifu/yoro_railway.webp)
* **Infrastructure Features:** A regional transit network operating specialized "Cycle Trains," allowing passengers to board standard passenger cars with non-folded bicycles without supplementary freight fees. The transit corporation provides integrated bicycle rental points at core stations alongside pre-mapped navigational routing guides covering historical itineraries, including the Tado Taisha Shrine and Sekigahara Battlefield courses.
* **Specialized Rolling Stock:** Runs periodic, non-scheduled thematic event trains, most notably the "Masuzake Train," which serves regional sake inside traditional square wooden boxes (Masu) manufactured via Ogaki's industry-leading woodwright factories.

## 🍛 Gourmet

### 🍡 Mizu-Manju (Artesian Water Confectionery)
![Mizu-Manju](/images/prefectures/gifu/mizu_manju.webp)
* **Featured Shop: Kinchoen Sohonke (Ogaki Ekimae)**
    * **Profile:** Established in 1798, this historical confectionery house optimizes Ogaki’s high-purity artesian water network to manufacture traditional wagashi. The signature product, Mizu-Manju, is a translucent, gelled dumpling made with arrowroot and kuzu starch, chilled directly under running spring water, and filled with sweet bean paste. Seasonal products include premium strawberry-infused rice cakes (Ichigo-mochi).
