## 🏛️ Introduction

* **A Destination for Food Travelers:** Travelers who have fully indulged in the gourmet food of Fukuoka and Hakata should head next to the northwestern edge of Saga Prefecture, the port town of "Yobuko." Hidden here is an outrageous street food that will fundamentally overturn all your previous notions of food. That is the unbelievably, completely transparent "Yobuko Live Squid" (Live Squid).
* **An Epicurean Adventure:** Just pulled from the nets, the squid still twitches on the plate, crystal clear. The moment it enters your mouth, the crisp, snappy texture and intense sweetness present a true culinary adventure. Weaving through the bustle of the "Yobuko Morning Market," which has continued since the Taisho era, let us set out on the ultimate food hack to slam the pure blessings of the world's finest ocean and the local energy of the market aunties straight into your stomach.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### ⛰️ Kagamiyama View
![Kagamiyama View](/images/prefectures/saga/karatsu_kagamiyama_view.webp)
* **Topography & Views:** Mount Kagami stands at an elevation of **284 meters**. The summit observation deck overlooks urban Karatsu, the Niji-no-Matsubara pine grove, Karatsu Bay, and the distant silhouette of Iki Island. A winding **4-kilometer** road connects the base to the summit area.
* **Historical Lore:** The mountain is documented in the *Man'yoshu* anthology and is associated with the legend of Matsura Sayohime, who allegedly waved a part of her cloth (*hire*) from the summit to bid farewell to Otomo no Sadehiko, giving the mountain its alternative name, Hirefuriyama. The summit park features public rest facilities, open lawns, and seasonal cherry blossoms and azaleas.

### 🌲 Niji-no-Matsubara Pine Grove
![Niji-no-Matsubara](/images/prefectures/saga/karatsu_niji_no_matsubara.webp)
* **Site Dimensions:** A registered National Place of Scenic Beauty stretching approximately **4.5 kilometers** in length and **0.5 kilometers** in width along the shoreline of Karatsu Bay. It stands as one of Japan's three major registered pine groves.
* **Botanical Composition & History:** The grove consists of approximately 1 million *kuromatsu* (black pine) trees. Cultivation originated in the early 17th century when Terasawa Hirotaka, the inaugural lord of the Karatsu Domain, planted the trees along coastal sand dunes to function as a barrier against wind and tidal erosion.

### 🏯 Karatsu Castle
![Karatsu Castle](/images/prefectures/saga/karatsu_castle.webp)
* **Historical Origins:** Initial construction began in 1602 by Terasawa Shimanokami Hirotaka, taking seven years to complete. The original structure utilized Mitsushimayama, a land-tied island bounded by rivers and the ocean, as its inner citadel (*honmaru*). The fort was decommissioned in 1871 following the abolition of the feudal domain system, and the site became Maizuru Park.
* **Current Structure & Exhibits:** The current 5-story main castle tower was constructed in 1966 as a cultural tourism facility. Its interior displays historical weapons, domain-era records, archaeological specimens, and historical Karatsu-ware pottery items. The 5th floor serves as an open observation platform overlooking the Genkai Sea.
* **Park Surroundings:** Maizuru Park contains seasonal cherry blossoms (late March) and wisteria displays (late April). The castle structure features exterior illumination at night.
* **Official Website:** https://karatsujo.com/

### 🛍️ Karatsu Furusato Kaikan Arpino
* **Facility Layout:** A multi-story regional product center located near the railway station.
  * **1st Floor (Karatsu Product Exhibition Hall):** Retails regional specialty foods including *Shoro Manju*, *Matsuura-zuke* pickles, *Ika-shumai* (steamed squid dumplings), and seafood sourced from the Genkai Sea.
  * **2nd Floor (Karatsu-yaki Ceramic Exhibition Hall):** Showcases and retails functional ceramic wares produced by 12 member kilns operating within the Karatsu area, reflecting a historic local potting lineage spanning over 400 years. The facility accommodates reservations for interactive ceramic painting experiences (*etsuke*).

## 🥢 Local Gourmet & Markets

### 🦑 Yobuko Morning Market
![Yobuko Squid](/images/prefectures/saga/karatsu_yobuko_squid.webp)
* **Market Details:** Operational since the Taisho era, it stands as one of Japanese three major traditional morning markets alongside Wajima (Ishikawa) and Takayama (Gifu). It features temporary open-air stalls set up along a **200-meter-long** street on the eastern flank of Yobuko Port.
* **Available Commodities:** Local vendors retail freshly caught marine products, processed seafood goods, vegetables, and flowers. Squid dishes represent the primary local culinary specialty.

### 🥮 Ohara Shoro Manju (Karatsu Main Shop)
* **Product Description:** A premier traditional confection of Karatsu, consisting of smooth sweet red bean paste wrapped in a sponge cake (*castella*) exterior, formed by hand into spherical shapes. The confections are packed in boxes featuring artwork of the regional Karatsu Kunchi festival. Freshly baked items arrive at the shop daily around 10:00.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

I highly recommend basing yourself in Hakata, taking the train out to Karatsu Station, and putting a rental car to full use for a day trip. The Yobuko Morning Market is located in a bit of a different direction compared to the rest of Karatsu City, but the squid there is truly exquisite. From my perspective, this squid is so highly recommended that it's worth going to Karatsu just to eat it, so you should definitely plan your schedule to arrive at Karatsu Station in the morning, rent a car, and head straight to the morning market for your meal. After that, the ideal route is to head back toward downtown Karatsu to hit up the various sightseeing spots, return your rental car early, and take a stroll around Karatsu Station before heading back to Hakata. By the way, even if you aren't basing your trip out of Hakata, a day trip is completely feasible using Saga City as your starting point instead.
