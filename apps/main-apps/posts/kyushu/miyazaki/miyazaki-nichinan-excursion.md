## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Welcome to California in Japan:** If you want "endless blue ocean, swaying palm trees, and an amazing feeling of freedom" for your trip to Japan, you should drive south from Miyazaki without hesitation. Waiting for you there is "Nichinan," an area full of tropical vibes often called "Japan's California."
* **A Wild Power Spot:** A road trip along the Nichinan Coast, driving with the endless Pacific Ocean on your left while looking at the wavy, strange rocks called the "Devil's Washboard," is a highlight of the trip by itself. But past this refreshing route lies a super mystery spot found nowhere else in the world: "Udo Shrine." It is built literally snugly inside a huge cave on a cliff hit by the rough waves of the Pacific Ocean. Let's go hack this wild power spot where dynamic natural power and faith mix together, completely opposite to traditional Japanese quietness.

## 💡 Author’s Tips

I highly recommend doing a day trip by renting a car from downtown Miyazaki. If you do not base yourself in downtown Miyazaki, the travel time from other big cities will be too long and probably very tiring. Nichinan City is quite large, so if you try to travel using only local buses, your trip will be rushed by the clock. There are many hotels and places to stay in downtown Miyazaki, so it is best to arrive early and stay there for one night, rent a car the next day, and then head out to Nichinan City.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🐴 Komamiya Shrine
![Komamiya Shrine](/images/prefectures/miyazaki/nichinan_komamiya_shrine.webp)
* **History:** This shrine is known as the childhood home of Emperor Jimmu, the first Emperor of Japan. 
* **Local History:** In the old days, a horse office sat 4 kilometers north in Tateishi, where Emperor Jimmu once released his favorite horse. People brought their best horses decorated with colorful cloths and bells to the shrine's autumn festival to pray for healthy horses and good crops.

### 🦁 Lion Rock & Gion Shrine (Umegahama)
![Lion Rock](/images/prefectures/miyazaki/nichinan_lion_rock.webp)
* **Lion Rock:** A famous large rock shaped like a lion sitting on the coast along Route 220, next to the Umegahama surfing spot.
* **Gion Shrine:** Located inside a cold cave just across the road from Lion Rock. The cave shrine houses the Dragon God (*Yamata-no-Orochi*) and the Water God (*Mizuhanome-no-Mikoto*), who protect people from water accidents, sickness, and stomach aches.

### 🗿 Sunmesse Nichinan
![Moai at Sunmesse Nichinan](/images/prefectures/miyazaki/nichinan_moai.webp)
* **The Moai Statues:** This park has 7 Moai statues (*Ahu Akivi*) in the world officially allowed to be copied by the Easter Island elders. This was a thank-you gift after a Japanese team spent 3 years (from 1992) fixing 15 broken original statues on Easter Island. Each statue is 5.5 meters tall and weighs 18 to 20 tons.
* **Other Facilities:** The park also includes a world insect exhibition, UNESCO information panels, a large butterfly picture drawn on the ground, a shop, a restaurant, and fast-food stands.
* **Official Website:** https://sun-messe.co.jp/

### 🏯 Obi Castle Town
![Obi Castle Town](/images/prefectures/miyazaki/nichinan_obi_castle_town.webp)
* **History:** Obi was a busy castle town of the Ito clan for 280 years from 1588 until the early Meiji period. The area has old samurai gates and stone walls. It was picked as a National Important Preservation District for Groups of Traditional Buildings in 1977.
* **Main Sights:** You can see the Otemon Gate (rebuilt in 1978), Matsuo-no-maru, Shintokudo School, and the Obi Castle History Museum. The old merchant street has houses decorated with wooden lattices, wall lanterns, and paper umbrellas, next to a clean river canal.
* **Official Website:** https://obijyo.com/

### ⛩️ Udo Shrine
![Udo Shrine](/images/prefectures/miyazaki/nichinan_udo_shrine.webp)
* **The Main Hall:** A bright red shrine hall built completely inside a large cave on the edge of Cape Udo. The cape is surrounded by sharp ocean rocks and rough waves of the Pacific Ocean.
* **Lucky Stones (Undama):** Visitors can test their luck by throwing small clay balls called *Undama* into a small hole on a rock shaped like a turtle (called Kameishi). Men throw with their left hand, and women throw with their right hand to make a wish come true.

## 🥢 Local Gourmet

### 🐟 Flame-Seared Bonito
![Flame-Seared Bonito](/images/prefectures/miyazaki/nichinan_flame_seared_bonito.webp)
* **Food Concept:** Nichinan City catches the most single-line hook bonito (*Katsuo*) in Japan. This local dish lets you cook fresh bonito on a small grill at your table.

**Restaurant: \"[Bibinya](https://www.bibinya.com/)\"** To keep the pure fish flavor, they mix the bonito with special oils right before serving instead of soaking it for a long time. They offer two tastes: one with olive oil and one with sesame oil, making it easy to eat even if it is half-raw.
