## 🏛️ Introduction

* **The Absolute Holy Land:** "Beppu" in Oita Prefecture boasts the highest volume of hot spring water discharge in Japan. However, the purpose of travelers from all over the world who visit here is not just to soak in the hot springs and relax.
* **The Seven Wonders:** In this area, where eerie plumes of steam rise from various spots across the town, there exist seven bizarre landscapes fiercely spouting hot mud and boiling water near 100℃ where humans cannot step foot—commonly known as the "Beppu Hells Tour" (Hells Tour).
* **The Wonderland:** A deathly pond glowing beautifully in cobalt blue, a soup stained literally blood-red just like the Blood Pond Hell, and psychedelic sights of grey mud bubbling eerily make this a true wonderland where the Earth is stripped bare.

## 📍 Picked Up Highlights & Landmarks

### 🌊 Sea Hell (Umi Jigoku)
![Sea Hell](/images/prefectures/oita/beppu_sea_hell.webp)
* **Historical Background:** Formed approximately 1,300 years ago during an eruption of Mount Tsurumi. The site is officially designated as a National Place of Scenic Beauty.
* **Physical Features:** The water displays a cobalt blue coloration due to dissolved iron sulfate. The thermal spring maintains a temperature of **98°C** with a verified depth of **200 meters**. The surrounding enclosure features a landscaped botanical garden.
* **Facilities & Products:** Features an on-site retail shop selling "Enman-no-yu," a proprietary bath salt powder derived from the local hot spring water.

### 🪵 Kamado Hell (Kamado Jigoku)
![Kamado Hell](/images/prefectures/oita/beppu_kamado_hell.webp)
* **Structural Layout:** The facility is divided into six distinct thermal zones labeled from 1st to 6th. Some ponds show variations in water color depending on atmospheric temperature and weather conditions.
* **Experiential Facilities:** Equipped with interactive thermal features including public footbaths and dedicated hot spring drinking stations.
* **Local Culinary Options:** On-site food stalls sell regional thermal specialties including "Onsen Pitan" (steamed hot spring eggs), "Ishigaki Manju" (sweet potato buns), and soy sauce puddings.

### 🐊 Crocodile Hell (Oniyama Jigoku)
![Crocodile Hell](/images/prefectures/oita/beppu_crocodile_hell.webp)
* **Operational History:** Established in 1923, this site was the first facility in Japan to utilize geothermal hot spring heat for crocodile breeding.
* **Biological Exhibits:** The facility houses approximately 80 crocodiles. The exhibit room displays a taxidermy specimen of the historical crocodile "Ichiro" and biological specimens of the endangered gharial (*gavial*).

### 💧 White Pond Hell (Shiraike Jigoku)
![White Pond Hell](/images/prefectures/oita/beppu_white_pond_hell.webp)
* **Site Features:** Opened in 1931, the facility centers around a traditional Japanese-style garden. The grounds house two registered regional cultural properties: the Kunisaki Tower (*Kunisaki-to*) and the Mukobaru Stone Pillar (*Mukobaru-sekido*).
* **Aquarium Facility:** Includes an integrated tropical fish conservatory powered by geothermal steam heat, displaying rare fish species including *pirarucu* and piranhas.

### 🏘️ Kannawa Hot Spring District
![Geothermal Cooking](/images/prefectures/oita/beppu_geothermal_cooking.webp)
* **District Character:** A historic thermal commercial district characterized by continuous geothermal steam emissions.
* **Infrastructure:** The area features a variety of cafés, restaurants, retail shops, and take-out stores. Visitors can access local maps and discount coupons at the municipal tourist information center.
* **Geothermal Cooking & Baths:** Features public facilities for *Jigoku-mushi* (geothermal steam cooking). Public footbaths are available near the steam workshop, alongside heated stone bed facilities located within Otani Park.

### 🩸 Blood Pond Hell (Chinoike Jigoku)
![Blood Pond Hell](/images/prefectures/oita/beppu_blood_pond_hell.webp)
* **Historical Context:** Documented as Japan's oldest natural thermal mud pool, characterized by an accumulation of red-colored, high-temperature acidic mud minerals.
* **Observations & Amenities:** An elevated observation and photography deck is accessible via the staircase located to the left of the on-site ointment retail stall. The grounds feature a large-scale public footbath.
* **Specialty Products:** The local shop produces and sells "Chinoike Ointment" (a specialized skin ointment formulated using the local mineral mud) and "Chinoike Pudding."

## 💡 Author’s Tips

If you have a rental car, you can do the tour in about 2.5 hours, but since there are buses running, you might as well use them. If you plan to do the tour via bus and walking, it is safer to budget around 3 to 3.5 hours (taking into account bus waiting times, etc.). As you know, this is an ultra-popular tourist destination, so it gets incredibly crowded. If you wish to stay overnight, you should definitely book your accommodations well in advance.
