## 🏛️ Introduction

* **Geographic Profile:** Located at the northernmost point of the "Another Kyoto" coastal region in Kyotango City. The coastline belongs to the Coast San'in Coast National Park / UNESCO Global Geopark.
* **Regional Infrastructure:** The coastal roads are utilized for driving and cycling tours around the Tango Peninsula. 
* **Accommodation and Food:** The area has numerous inns that serve regional seafood, vegetables, and traditional local cuisine linked to the regional climate. It also connects directly to other major sightseeing destinations in northern Kansai.

## 📍 Highlights & Landmarks

### 🏠 Inaba Honke Merchant Residence
![Inaba Honke Residence](/images/prefectures/kyoto/kyotango_inaba_honke_residence.webp)
* **Overview:** A restored historical residence of the Inaba family, who accumulated wealth through koji manufacturing and coastal shipping commerce during the Edo period. The main building is 134 years old and registered as a National Registered Tangible Cultural Property. 
* **Facilities:** The Ginshosha building is approximately 220 years old, where local Botamochi rice sweets are prepared and sold next to a traditional garden. The facility hosts pottery and incense-making workshops and sells regional Kyotango souvenirs. Paid guided tours of the surrounding castle town are available.

### 🌊 Kumihama Bay
![Kumihama Bay](/images/prefectures/kyoto/kyotango_kumihama_bay.webp)
* **Overview:** A lagoon with a circumference of 28 kilometers, separated from the Sea of Japan by a sandbar. The calm inner waters contain oyster cultivation racks, and the area is designated as a Cultural Landscape of Kyoto Prefecture.
* **Product Features:** The high concentration of plankton supports the cultivation of thick local "Kumihama Oysters." Observation and photography are typically conducted from Kumihama Park or the Kabutoyama Observatory.
* **Address:** Kumihama-cho, Kyotango City, Kyoto.

### 🎨 Wakuden MORI
* **Overview:** A cultural facility displaying delicate watercolor paintings by Mitsumasa Anno, housed in a building designed by architect Tadao Ando. 
* **Facilities:** Features the "wakuden MORI" workshop restaurant, which serves meals and refreshments using ingredients collected from the local forest and Tango region. A museum shop sells limited-edition goods featuring Mitsumasa Anno's artwork.
* **Official Website:** https://mori.wakuden.kyoto/

### 🏖️ Kotohikihama Beach
![Kotohikihama Beach](/images/prefectures/kyoto/kyotango_kotohikihama.webp)
* **Overview:** A scenic coastal strip spanning 1.8 kilometers, designated as a National Natural Monument and Place of Scenic Beauty. It is known as a singing sand beach where the friction of quartz particles creates a distinct sound when walked upon.
* **Environmental Rules:** Because the sand only produces sound when kept clean, local community volunteers conduct regular maintenance. Visitors must bring home their trash and adhere to the strict no-smoking policy.
* **Address:** Kakeszu, Amino-cho, Kyotango City, Kyoto.

## 🍽️ Local Gourmet

### 🦪 Huuran no Yakata Oyster Shack
![Furan no Yata](/images/prefectures/kyoto/kyotango_oyster.webp)
* **Seasonal Schedule:** Sells fresh local rock oysters from Kumihama Bay between June and October. From November to May, the facility operates as an all-you-can-eat oyster shack.
* **Menu Items:** The main set menu include options containing raw rock oysters, steamed oysters, oyster rice, and deep-fried oysters.
* **Official Website:** https://www.huuran.com/

## 💡 Author’s Tips

* A 2-day, 1-night trip is the most efficient, and getting around by rental car is highly recommended. Kyotango City shows you a completely different side from the typical "Kyoto"—honestly, you won't get bored even if you stay for two nights. 
* Spending two nights here like a vacation, relaxing at a ryokan inn and moving at your own pace with a rental car, makes for the ultimate chill time.
